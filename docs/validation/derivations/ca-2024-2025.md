## Rules applied to every scenario

1. **Federal AGI** = wages (1040 line 1a/1z) plus capital gain (line 7 / 7a),
   no Schedule 1 adjustments. 2025 line 11a/11b; 2024 line 11. The 2025 federal
   Schedule 1-A deductions (including the senior deduction) enter at 1040 line
   13b, below AGI, so they do not change Form 540 line 13.
2. **California adjustments** (lines 14 and 16) = 0. Schedule CA instructions:
   lines 1a-1z, "Generally, you will not make any adjustments on these lines";
   line 7/7a, "Generally, no adjustments are made on this line. California
   taxes long and short term capital gains as regular income. No special rate
   for long term capital gains exists." Assumes ordinary W-2 wages and no
   federal/California basis difference.
3. Line 17 = line 15 = line 13. Line 18 = standard deduction. Line 19 = line 17
   − line 18.
4. Line 31 from the Tax Table when line 19 ≤ 100,000, otherwise the Tax Rate
   Schedule.
5. Line 32 = line 11 unless line 13 exceeds the threshold, then the AGI
   Limitation Worksheet.
6. Line 33 = line 31 − line 32 (not below zero). Line 35 = line 33. No credits
   on lines 40-46 are claimed (see Assumptions), so line 48 = line 35.
7. Line 61 (AMT) = 0, checked against Schedule P for every scenario (below).
8. Line 62 = 1% × (line 19 − 1,000,000) when line 19 > 1,000,000.
9. Line 64 = line 48 + line 61 + line 62 + line 63.

### Rounding rules, as found

- Form 540 money lines are pre-printed ".00": whole dollars.
- Tax Table amounts are whole dollars as printed.
- Tax Rate Schedule (R25/R24, "How to Figure Tax", Step 4): round the product
  to two decimals, add the base tax, then round the result to a whole dollar
  (example: 4,768.10 → 4,768; 2024: 4,920.48 → 4,920).
- AGI Limitation Worksheet: "Use whole dollars only"; line d, "If the result is
  not a whole number, round it to the next higher whole number."
- Line 62 worksheet: "using whole dollars only".
- The booklet text retrieved contains no general "50 cents and over rounds up"
  sentence. Nearest-dollar, half-up rounding is assumed where a final amount
  has cents. Only line 62 of scenario 10 depends on it materially (see
  Uncertainties). No Tax Rate Schedule result here falls on .50.

### Assumptions

- Full-year residents; nobody can be claimed as a dependent by another
  (line 6 unchecked); not blind; under 65 except scenario 7.
- Dependents are qualifying dependents listed on the federal return.
- No nonrefundable renter's credit (line 46), child and dependent care credit
  (line 40), or other credits are claimed. The renter's credit depends on
  facts not given (renting, AGI limits). Refundable credits (CalEITC, YCTC,
  FYTC) are payments on lines 75-77, after line 64, and are out of scope.
- No use tax, individual shared responsibility penalty, or other line 63/91/92
  amounts.
- Scenario 4 and 5 "one earner" does not affect a California joint return.

## Uncertainties

1. **Line 62 rounding (scenario 10).** The worksheet says "whole dollars only"
   but the retrieved booklet text does not say how to round the 1% product.
   Nearest dollar is assumed: 1,942.94 → 1,943 (2025) and 1,944.60 → 1,945
   (2024). Truncation would give 1,942 and 1,944, lowering total tax by $1 in
   each year. No official statement was found to settle this.
2. **General rounding statement.** No general "round to the nearest whole
   dollar, 50 cents up" instruction was located in the 2024 or 2025 Form 540
   booklet text; the rule is inferred from the ".00" lines and the Tax Rate
   Schedule example. No other figure here sits near a half-dollar.
3. **Scenario 9, 2025.** As specified it does not trigger the exemption
   phase-out (500,000 < 504,411). This is a property of the scenario, not an
   unverified value. Scenario 9b was added to cover a 2025 phase-out.
4. **Page numbers.** Booklet page numbers are not recorded for most items
   (the HTML booklet has none); items are identified by form line and
   worksheet name instead. The 2024 Tax Rate Schedules are booklet page 75.
5. **Tax Table transcription.** Rows were read from `pdftotext -layout` output
   of the official PDFs, not by eye from the rendered page. The midpoint
   cross-check above supports all twelve values.
6. **Credits not modelled.** Nonrefundable renter's credit, child and dependent
   care credit, and refundable CalEITC/YCTC are excluded by assumption; a real
   filer in scenarios 1 or 6 might qualify for some of them.
7. **IRS 2025 Form 1040 URL.** Retrieved from the `irs-prior` path, which
   returned the 2025 form; the line 11a/11b split matches Form 540 line 13's
   reference to "line 11b".
