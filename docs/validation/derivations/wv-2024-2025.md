# West Virginia IT-140, tax years 2024 and 2025 — independent derivation

Blind derivation, 2026-11-11, by a Claude subagent. Sources: the West Virginia
Tax Division's official IT-140 booklets (forms, instructions, tax table, rate
schedules) and IRS Form 1040, fetched from the web on 2026-11-11. No tenforty
code, spec, table, graph, test or fixture was read (only
`docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md`, for the evidence format), and no
tax software or calculator was run. A throwaway script parsed the official
table text to locate rows; every row used below was then read back from the
PDF text and is transcribed in full.

All scenarios: full-year resident, cannot be claimed as a dependent, no
credits, no Schedule M modifications except where stated. "Tax" is Form IT-140
**line 8** (income tax before the line 9 credits and before withholding).

### Rules relied on (identical wording in WV24 and WV25 unless noted)

- **Form IT-140, lines 1–8** (form page 1, PDF p. 3):
  line 1 federal AGI; line 2 additions (Schedule M); line 3 subtractions
  (Schedule M); line 4 "West Virginia Adjusted Gross Income (line 1 plus line 2
  minus line 3)"; line 5 Low-Income Earned Income Exclusion; line 6 "Total
  Exemptions as shown above on Exemption Box (e) ____ x $2,000"; line 7 "West
  Virginia Taxable Income (line 4 minus lines 5 & 6) IF LESS THAN ZERO, ENTER
  ZERO"; line 8 "Income Tax Due (Check One)": Tax Table / Rate Schedule /
  Nonresident-Part-year schedule.
- **No standard or itemized deduction.** Lines 1–7 are the complete path from
  federal AGI to taxable income; the only subtractions are Schedule M
  modifications (line 3), the low-income exclusion (line 5) and exemptions
  (line 6).
- **Exemptions** (p. 24, "EXEMPTIONS"; also p. 19): "You can deduct $2,000 for
  every exemption claimed in your exemption schedule." Box (a) yourself, (b)
  spouse on a joint return, (c) dependents, (d) surviving spouse, (e) total.
  $500 if box (e) is zero. There is no extra exemption for age 65.
- **Line 5, Low-Income Earned Income Exclusion Worksheet** (p. 29): eligible
  only if federal AGI is "$10,000 or less" ($5,000 MFS); "If Line A is greater
  than $10,000 ... you are not eligible for the exclusion. STOP HERE";
  otherwise the smaller of federal AGI, earned income, or $10,000.
- **Line 8 method** (pp. 24–25): filing status single, head of household,
  widow(er) or married filing jointly "and your taxable income is less than
  $100,000, apply the amount of taxable income shown on line 7 to the Tax Table
  on page 36"; "over $100,000, use Rate Schedule I on page 35". Married filing
  separately must use Rate Schedule II.
- **Senior citizen deduction** (Schedule M, PDF p. 6; instructions p. 27):
  2024 **line 49**, 2025 **line 47** (the schedule was renumbered; total
  subtractions are line 52 in 2024 and line 50 in 2025). "Taxpayers MUST be at
  least age 65 ..."; "Box (c) Enter all income ... not reported on lines 35
  through 48" — box (c) is headed "(NOT TO EXCEED $8000)"; "Box (d) Add lines
  29 through 34"; the deduction is (c) minus (d), not below zero. The printed
  example (p. 28) counts "Wages and Salaries" in Mary Doe's total income for
  box (c). Filing-requirement text (p. 15): "$2,000 per exemption plus up to
  $8,000 of income received by each taxpayer who is 65 or older."
- **Rounding** (p. 15, "Round off amounts to WHOLE DOLLARS – NO CENTS"; p. 35
  rate-schedule example, "Round to nearest whole dollar"). The Tax Table prints
  whole-dollar tax.
- **Tax Table instructions** (p. 36): "Make sure your taxable income is LESS
  than and NOT equal to the income shown in the 'LESS THAN' column"; "If your
  taxable income is over $100,000 refer to the Tax Rate Schedules on page 35."
  Rows are "At Least / But Less Than"; bands are $100 wide from $100 to
  $25,000, $60 wide from $25,000 to $40,000, and $50 wide from $40,000 to
  $100,000. The last row is 99,950–100,000.

### Federal inputs

Form 1040 (IRS24, IRS25): wages on line 1a/1z flow to line 9 total income;
with no Schedule 1 adjustments, line 11 AGI equals line 9. Capital gain is
line 7 and is part of line 9, so scenario 11's federal AGI is
60,000 + 10,000 = 70,000. The preferential federal rate on long-term gains
affects federal tax only, not AGI. No federal deduction (standard deduction,
or the 2025 enhanced senior deduction) is above the line, so none changes AGI.
These line references are from the deriver's knowledge of the form; the IRS
PDFs were confirmed reachable but not re-read (Uncertainty 5).

## Uncertainties

1. **Scenario 1 is not an exclusion case.** The brief labels it "low-income
   earned income exclusion", but the worksheet (p. 29, both years) bars the
   exclusion when federal AGI exceeds $10,000. At wages of 12,000 line 5 is 0.
   A scenario that exercises the exclusion needs wages of 10,000 or less, and
   there taxable income is 0 (exclusion equals AGI). Not derived here because
   it was not asked for.
2. **Senior deduction applied to wages (scenario 10).** The instruction says
   box (c) is "all income" not on the other subtraction lines, the printed
   example includes wages in total income, and the filing-requirement text says
   "up to $8,000 of income". That supports the 8,000 subtraction for a
   wage-only senior. The statute (W. Va. Code §11-21-12) was not fetched, so
   the reading rests on the booklet alone. Year of birth is assumed to make the
   taxpayer 67 at year end in each year (1957 for 2024, 1958 for 2025).
3. **Taxable income of exactly $100,000 is unaddressed.** The table covers
   "less than $100,000" and the schedule is directed for "over $100,000"; the
   table's last row excludes 100,000. No scenario lands there (scenario 7 is at
   96,000). The table/schedule switch is also discontinuous: 99,950–100,000
   prints $4,230 (2024) / $3,980 (2025), while the schedule at 100,000 gives
   4,231.50 / 3,981.50.
4. **Table versus schedule below $100,000.** The instructions direct the Tax
   Table for these filers; the table value differs from the exact-income
   schedule by up to about $2 because it is computed at the band midpoint
   (largest gap here: scenario 1, 238 vs 236.00 in 2024). An implementation
   that applies the schedule to exact income will not match lines 8 above
   except by coincidence. The booklet does not say the schedule is an
   acceptable alternative below $100,000.
5. **Federal AGI** rests on the Form 1040 line structure from the deriver's
   knowledge; the IRS PDFs were confirmed reachable on 2026-11-11 but not
   opened. The claim is only that wages and capital gain are both in AGI with
   no above-the-line adjustment, which is not in doubt.
6. **Booklet inconsistencies noticed, none affecting a value.** The 2025 form
   face still says subtractions come from "line 52 of Schedule M" and additions
   from "line 61", though the 2025 Schedule M totals subtractions on line 50;
   the 2025 line 47 instruction still says "lines 35 through 48" where the form
   says "35 to 46". The 2024 booklet p. 19 has a typo, "page 4124".
7. **Dependents** are assumed to be qualifying dependents under the federal
   rules West Virginia retains (p. 19), and the head-of-household filer is
   assumed to qualify for that status. No child or family credit is applied:
   the figures are line 8, before the line 9 credit recap (including the Family
   Tax Credit, which has income limits and was not evaluated).
8. **Booklet revision.** The PDFs are identified by the sha256 above; whether
   the Tax Division has issued a later revision of either booklet was not
   checked.
