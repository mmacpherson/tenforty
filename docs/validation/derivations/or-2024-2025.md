## Federal tax liability subtraction

OR-40 line 10: take the smaller of eligible federal liability and Table 4 cap.
Eligible liability starts at federal line 22 (after nonrefundable credits),
subtracts excess advance premium credit repayment, floors at zero, adds the
specified income-tax-only Schedule 2 amounts, then subtracts AOTC and total
premium credit (and refundable adoption credit in 2025), floors at zero.
EITC is not subtracted. Self-employment tax is not included.

Published Table 4 rows (lower bound inclusive, upper exclusive):

| AGI S/MFS | AGI MFJ/HOH/QSS | 2024 S or J | 2024 MFS | 2025 S or J | 2025 MFS |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| below 125,000 | below 250,000 | 8,250 | 4,125 | 8,500 | 4,250 |
| 125,000–130,000 | 250,000–260,000 | 6,600 | 3,300 | 6,800 | 3,400 |
| 130,000–135,000 | 260,000–270,000 | 4,950 | 2,475 | 5,100 | 2,550 |
| 135,000–140,000 | 270,000–280,000 | 3,300 | 1,650 | 3,400 | 1,700 |
| 140,000–145,000 | 280,000–290,000 | 1,650 | 825 | 1,700 | 850 |
| at least 145,000 | at least 290,000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

For every status and both years, test each listed boundary minus $1, at the
boundary, and plus $1, with stipulated eligible federal liability $50,000:
the subtraction is exactly the corresponding cap. With liability $100 the
subtraction is min(100, cap); with zero liability it is zero. These cases
separate liability limitation from AGI limitation. The phase-out does not
interpolate within an interval.

## Exemption credit

OR-40 line 25: $249 (2024) or $256 (2025) times qualifying exemption count.
Regular exemptions: taxpayer plus spouse on MFJ, plus dependents. QSS does
not grant an exemption for the deceased spouse. HOH/QSS dependents count
separately. A dependent taxpayer cannot claim their own regular exemption.
MFS may also claim a spouse without income who cannot be claimed elsewhere.
Severe-disability and qualifying-disabled-child exemptions are additional,
with a separate $100,000 AGI ceiling for all statuses.

Regular count is allowed through AGI $100,000 inclusive for S/MFS and $200,000
inclusive for MFJ/HOH/QSS. Above that ceiling it is zero, with no gradual phaseout.
For each year and each status test ceiling minus $1, ceiling, ceiling plus $1.
For zero dependents, regular count S/MFS/HOH/QSS is 1 and MFJ is 2:
credits 249/498 in 2024; 256/512 in 2025. Add one credit per dependent.
The credit is nonrefundable: tax after credits cannot go below zero.

## Published-table arithmetic equivalence

An arithmetic comparison against all 1,004 transcribed rows (both S/J columns)
found zero differences for this rule: for [0,20) use zero, [20,50) use two,
[50,100) use four; thereafter use the midpoint of each $100 interval, multiply
its income in each official marginal bracket by that bracket's rate, round
EACH increment half up to a whole dollar, then sum. This comparison used only
the DOR CSV transcriptions, the official thresholds/rates above, and Python
Decimal with ROUND_HALF_UP, with no tenforty code or outputs. The spec uses
this equivalent compact arithmetic; the tests still compare every published
row directly rather than recomputing expectations from that rule.

For example, 2024 J midpoint 21,550: round(8,600×.0475)=409;
round(12,900×.0675)=871; round(50×.0875)=4; sum=1,284.
2025 J midpoint 22,250: round(8,800×.0475)=418;
round(13,400×.0675)=905; round(50×.0875)=4; sum=1,327.
These are table calculations, distinct from the OR-40 chart used at $50,000.

## Supported surfaces and limits

The natural graph scalar and batch APIs derive the regular taxpayer/spouse
base and use num_dependents as the total regular dependent count. The generic
dollar-valued dependent_exemptions field remains unmapped for Oregon.
Additional exemptions, including severe-disability and qualifying-disabled-child
exemptions, claimable-taxpayer variants, and the MFS no-income-spouse exemption,
are unsupported in this change; no extra exemption inputs are introduced.

No new natural fields are introduced. At MFJ AGI 150,000, stipulating eligible
federal liability above the Table 4 cap even after dependent credits, two
dependents add 2×249=498 (2024) or 2×256=512 (2025) of Oregon credit without
changing Oregon taxable income. The current federal Schedule 8812 mapping still
treats the total count as qualifying children; separating federal dependent
eligibility is outside this Oregon change.

OTS 2024/2025 spouse mapping is repaired in models.py for tenforty-r91.6:
CkL6bRegular is Yes for MFJ and explicitly No for other statuses. The OR template
imports filing status from the federal return; this map supplies only its spouse
checkbox. No OTS dependent-count mapping is added. Vendored OTS is unchanged.
The existing strict-xfail spouse witness flipped to strict XPASS after this fix;
its marker is removed, and full Single/MFJ tax-parity coverage is restored without
an income-domain exclusion or any tolerance change.

Raw graph federal worksheet adjustments remain available. Full-year resident,
ordinary tax-method returns with base standard deductions are covered; special
tax methods, RDP/as-if or mixed-residency returns, amended-return worksheet
exceptions, and age/blind/dependent standard-deduction adjustments are unsupported.
