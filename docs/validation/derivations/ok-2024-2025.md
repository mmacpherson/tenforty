Blind derivation, 2026-11-11. Deriver: Claude subagent (Fable 5.2), fresh context.
Inputs used: the two official Oklahoma Tax Commission (OTC) 511 packets listed
under Sources, and nothing else. The only tenforty file read was
`docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md` (evidence format). No tenforty code,
spec, table, graph, test or fixture was read; no tax software or calculator was
run. The arithmetic below was done by hand from the packet text.

Full-year resident, Form 511, Oklahoma standard deduction, no out-of-state
income, no Schedule 511-A/B/C entries. "State tax" is Form 511 line 14
(Oklahoma income tax before credits, use tax and withholding).

## Federal inputs

Wages are the only income in scenarios 1-8, so federal AGI equals wages (no
adjustments to income). In scenario 9, the net long-term capital gain is
included in federal AGI in full, so federal AGI is 70,000. The federal
preferential rate on that gain affects federal tax only, not AGI. These follow
from the structure of Form 1040 (wages on line 1a, capital gain on line 7,
total income line 9, AGI line 11); IRS documents were not fetched for this
record because no federal figure other than AGI enters Form 511 here. Age (65+)
changes the federal standard deduction and, for 2025, the federal senior
deduction; both are below-AGI and do not change Form 511 line 1.

out-of-state income, no Schedule 511-B additions, no Schedule 511-C

### 9. Single, wages 60,000 plus 10,000 long-term capital gains

Assumption: the gain is from ordinary investment property (for example,
publicly traded stock of a company not headquartered in Oklahoma, or any asset
that fails the holding-period/location tests), so the Oklahoma Capital Gain
Deduction (Schedule 511-A line 12, Form 561) is zero. Oklahoma has no
preferential rate for capital gains; the gain is taxed as part of line 13.

## Credits that exist and are excluded

- **Oklahoma child care/child tax credit**, Form 511 line 15 (scenarios 6 and 7
  would be candidates). Not computed.
- **Oklahoma earned income credit**, 5% of the federal EIC, Form 511 line 28 /
  Schedule 511-G. Not computed.
- Also not considered: sales tax relief credit (Form 538-S), other credits
  (Form 511-CR), use tax (line 19), withholding and payments.

All "state tax" figures above are line 14, before any of these.

## Uncertainties

1. **Scenario 9 depends on a fact the scenario does not state.** Whether the
   10,000 gain qualifies for the Oklahoma Capital Gain Deduction turns on what
   was sold, where it is located or headquartered, and how long it was held.
   The pinned value assumes a non-qualifying gain. The two cases differ
   materially, so the fixture should state the assumption rather than treat it
   as a default.
2. **Table rows were read from `pdftotext -layout` output, not from the
   rendered page.** The column order was confirmed against the packet's own
   $14,793 joint example (325). As a transcription check only (not a source of
   any value), each row was compared with the packet's own worksheet anchor
   stepped back at 0.0475 from $100,000 to the row midpoint: single 4,562 less
   0.0475 x (100,000 - midpoint) gives 414, 1,364, 1,839, 2,789 and 3,501 to
   within the anchor's own rounding; MFJ/HOH 4,373 less the same step gives
   1,935, 3,581 and 3,676. All eight agree with the transcribed rows. A visual
   check of the eight rows against the PDF pages listed above has not been done.
3. **Page numbers for 2024 rows are inferred.** Page numbers in the rows table
   were established from the 2025 extraction's page footers; the 2024 extraction
   has the same rows at a constant ten-line offset and the same table of
   contents (Tax Table pp. 27-38), so the pages should match, but each 2024 row
   was not individually located by footer.
4. **No rate schedule was verified.** The packet publishes only the table and
   the $100,000-and-over worksheet. The bracket rates below 4.75% and their
   thresholds (which differ by filing status and, per the 2024 "What's New",
   were adjusted for the top two MFJ/HOH/surviving-spouse brackets in 2024) were
   not taken from an official statutory source and are not relied on here. Any
   test of taxable income under $100,000 should compare against the table row,
   not a formula.
5. **Federal AGI is stipulated, not sourced.** No IRS publication was fetched.
   Federal AGI = wages (plus the capital gain in scenario 9) assumes no
   adjustments to income.
6. **Identical 2024 and 2025 results rest on the 2025 packet as retrieved from
   the `/current/` URL** (ModDate 2025-12-02). If OTC reissued the 2025 packet
   after this retrieval, the sha256 above identifies the version used. The 2025
   "What's New" lists no rate, deduction or exemption change.
7. **Scenario 5/6 "one earner"** has no effect on Form 511 as read here; no
   spouse-specific adjustment was found in the line 10-14 instructions. The
   Schedule 511-A, B and C instructions were not read in full, only searched
   for items relevant to wages and capital gains.
8. **Dependents** are assumed to meet IRC Sec. 152 and to be claimed on the
   federal return, and no taxpayer is assumed claimable as a dependent or
   legally blind.
