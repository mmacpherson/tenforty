# Ohio IT 1040 — tax years 2024 and 2025 — independent derivation

## Header

- **Scope:** Ohio resident individual income tax (IT 1040), tax years 2024 and 2025.
  Ohio taxable nonbusiness income (IT 1040 line 5/7) and Ohio tax after nonrefundable
  credits (IT 1040 line 10), starting from a stipulated federal AGI. Wages only, plus one
  interest + LTCG case. Credits applied: exemption credit (Schedule of Credits line 9) and
  joint filing credit (Schedule of Credits line 12). Retirement, senior, lump-sum, child care,
  displaced worker, campaign contribution and all certificate credits omitted (zero).
  School district income tax (SD 100) ignored.

  form lines, booklet and Ohio Revised Code text below. A plain decimal calculator (Python
  `decimal`, written from scratch, implementing only the bracket formulas quoted below) was
  used afterwards solely to re-check the hand multiplication; every figure agreed.
- **Derived by:** Claude (Anthropic), model Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`), 2026-11-03.
- **Evidence kind for every value in this document:** Independent derivation.
- **Retrieval date for all sources:** 2026-11-03 (direct fetch; no 403, so no archive

## Law used

### Form line flow (F24/F25, identical structure both years)

- Line 1 federal AGI → line 2a additions (Sch. of Adjustments line 12) → line 2b deductions
  (Sch. of Adjustments line 46) → **line 3 Ohio AGI** = 1 + 2a − 2b.
- **MAGI** = line 3 + business income deduction (Sch. of Adjustments line 13) (B24/B25 p.8).
  No business income here, so MAGI = line 3.
- **Line 4** exemption amount = number of exemptions × per-exemption amount by MAGI.
- **Line 5** Ohio income tax base = line 3 − line 4 (not below 0).
- Line 6 taxable business income = 0 here. **Line 7 / 7a** taxable nonbusiness income = line 5 − line 6.
- **Line 8a** = bracket tax on line 7a; 8b = 0; **8c** = 8a.
- Schedule of Credits: line 1 = IT 1040 line 8c; line 9 exemption credit; line 10 = total of
  lines 2–9; **line 11** = line 1 − line 10 (not below 0); **line 12** joint filing credit =
  % × line 11, max $650; line 13 EIC; … line 39 total nonrefundable credits → IT 1040 line 9.
- **Line 10** tax after nonrefundable credits = 8c − 9 (not below 0).

**Ohio uses the bracket (rate) schedule, not a tax table.** Neither booklet contains a tax
table; Line 8a says "Calculate your tax … using the Income Tax Brackets found on page 18"
(with an optional online calculator). ORC 5747.02 contains no table provision.

### Exemption credit (B24/B25 p.29; R.C. 5747.022)

$20 × number of exemptions claimed, if **MAGI less exemptions < $30,000**. MAGI less
exemptions per the B24 p.40 worksheet = MAGI − line 4 (= line 5 for these returns).

### Joint filing credit (B24 p.29–30; B25 p.29; R.C. 5747.05(E))

MFJ only; each spouse must have ≥ $500 of qualifying income (Ohio AGI items other than
interest, dividends/distributions, capital gains, rents and royalties); 2025 additionally
requires MAGI < $750,000. Credit = percentage × Schedule of Credits line 11, max $650:

| MAGI less exemptions | % |
|---|---|
| 0 – $25,000 | 20% |
| $25,001 – $50,000 | 15% |
| $50,001 – $75,000 | 10% |
| $75,001 or more (2025: $75,001 – $749,999) | 5% |

### Ohio EIC (B24/B25 p.30; R.C. 5747.71)

30% of federal EIC. Stipulated federal EIC = $0 for every case (see assumptions), so
Schedule of Credits line 13 = 0 throughout.

## Rounding conventions

1. **Whole dollars on every form line.** F24/F25 page headers: "Use whole dollars only";
   B24 p.5 and B25 p.5 "Common Filing Tips": "Round all figures to the nearest dollar."
2. **Line 8a** is computed exactly from the bracket formula (the formula carries cents, e.g.
   $360.69) and then rounded to the nearest whole dollar before entry. Support: B25 p.18
   Mitchell worked example enters a whole-dollar $1,497 on line 8a (the exact value happens
   to be whole), and the form accepts whole dollars only.
3. **Joint filing credit** = % × line 11 (a whole-dollar figure), rounded to nearest dollar,
   then capped at $650.
4. **Half-dollar ties:** neither booklet states a tie rule. I round .50 **up** (half-up, the
   common federal convention "increase amounts from 50 to 99 cents to the next dollar").
   Two results depend on this; see Ambiguities A3.
5. The only rounding in ORC 5747.02 is (A)(5): indexed bracket amounts rounded to the
   nearest $50. It does not govern tax computation, and the bracket amounts above are the
   statutory figures for 2024/2025 as enacted (not indexed values).

## Assumptions

1. Federal AGI is a stipulated input equal to the stated wages (plus interest and LTCG in
   Case 22). No federal adjustments are modeled.
2. Full-year Ohio residents (both spouses for MFJ); no non-Ohio income, so no resident or
   nonresident credit.
3. No Ohio Schedule of Adjustments additions or deductions (line 2a = 2b = 0): no non-Ohio
   municipal interest, no U.S.-obligation interest, no 529, no medical, no state refund,
   no Social Security, etc.
4. No business income: wages, ordinary interest and portfolio LTCG are nonbusiness income;
   line 6 = 0, business income deduction = 0, so MAGI = Ohio AGI = federal AGI.
5. Exemption count: filer (+ spouse for MFJ) + stated dependents; nobody can be claimed as a
   dependent by another taxpayer.
6. All filers under 65; retirement, senior citizen, lump-sum credits omitted (zero) as
   instructed. No child care, displaced worker, campaign, or certificate credits.
7. Federal EIC stipulated as $0 for every case (no case has qualifying-child EIC in range,
   and every no-child case's AGI is above the federal no-child EIC phase-out). Ohio EIC = 0.
   This was not re-derived from IRS sources; it is a stipulation.
8. MFJ wage splits are as stated per case (both spouses ≥ $500 wages unless stated
   otherwise), so the joint filing credit is allowed except in Case 5b.
9. HoH treated as "Single, head of household or qualifying surviving spouse" on the IT 1040;
   Ohio has no separate HoH brackets.
10. School district income tax (SD 100) ignored; no interest penalty, no use tax.

## Ambiguities

- **A1 — Zero-bracket cliff (both years).** The statute and booklet put the first taxable
  bracket at "$360.69 [2025: $342.00] plus 2.75% of the amount in excess of $26,050" with
  0% "up to $26,050". Read literally, tax jumps from $0 at line 7 = $26,050 to $360.72 at
  $26,051. I apply it literally (Cases 14–15). An engine that treats $26,050 as a
  continuous 0% threshold (base $0) would differ by ~$361/$342 for every return above it.
  The B25 Mitchell worked example ($68,050 → $1,497 = $342 + $1,155) confirms the base is
  added in full, so the literal reading is the Department's.
- **A2 — 2025 discontinuity at $100,000.** ORC 5747.02(A)(3)(b) and B25 p.18 give a middle
  bracket ending at $342.00 + 2.75% × 73,950 = $2,375.625 but a top bracket starting at
  $2,394.32. I apply the text literally: line 7 = $100,000 → $2,376; $100,001 → $2,394
  (Cases 18–19). The 2024 schedule is continuous at $100,000 ($2,394.315 ≈ $2,394.32).
  Whether the boundary value $100,000 itself is in the middle bracket follows the
  "more than $100,000" wording for the top bracket.
- **A3 — Half-dollar ties.** Neither booklet nor form gives a tie rule; I round half up.
  Affected: 2025 Single $60,000 (exact $1,216.50 → $1,217) and 2025 Single $80,000
  (exact $1,766.50 → $1,767). Round-half-even or truncation would give $1,216 and $1,766.
  These two rows should be tested with a ±$1 tolerance or flagged; all other rows are
  not at a tie.
- **A4 — Whether line 8a is rounded before credits.** I round line 8a to whole dollars
  (form: "whole dollars only") and compute the joint filing credit on the whole-dollar
  line 11. Computing the JFC on the unrounded tax could change the credit by at most $1
  in some rows (e.g. Case 5 2024: 10% × 1,176.065 = 117.61 → 118, same result).
- **A5 — "MAGI less exemptions" for the exemption credit.** B24/B25 p.29 use MAGI less
  exemptions; with no business income deduction this equals line 5. The test is strict
  "less than $30,000" (Case 17 at exactly $30,000 gets no credit).
- **A6 — JFC band edges.** The table bands are written in whole dollars ("$50,001 –
  $75,000", "$75,001 or more"); with whole-dollar inputs there is no gap. $75,000 → 10%,
  $75,001 → 5% (Cases 20–21).
- **A7 — Exemption credit exceeding tax.** Schedule of Credits line 11 floors at zero, so an
  exemption credit larger than tax (Cases 1 and 14) is simply lost (nonrefundable).
- **A8 — Source versions.** The 2024 booklet used is the online revision (PDF created
  2025-04-10; corrections listed: p.40 worksheet reference only). The 2025 booklet used is
  the online revision dated "accurate as of November 27, 2025" (PDF created 2026-01-23);
  listed corrections do not touch brackets, exemptions or the two credits used here.
  ORC 5747.02 was read in its current (2025-09-30, H.B. 96) version, which still contains
  the 2024 division (A)(3)(a) text verbatim; earlier enacted versions (H.B. 33) were not
  separately fetched.
- **A9 — ORC sections not fetched.** R.C. 5747.025, 5747.022, 5747.05(E) and 5747.71 were
  relied on through the Department's restatement in the booklets, not read directly.
