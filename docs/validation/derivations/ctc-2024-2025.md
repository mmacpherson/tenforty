# Child Tax Credit: independent derivation, TY2024 and TY2025

- **Evidence kind:** independent derivation from official IRS forms and instructions. Tax line 16
  values are **published table rows** (Tax Table) or **published worksheet rows** (Tax Computation
  Worksheet).
- **Derived by:** Claude (Opus 5.5) subagent, blind. I did not read or run anything in the tenforty
  repo or worktrees, and I used no tax software (tenforty, Tax-Calculator, OpenTaxSolver).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-02, all documents from irs.gov (URLs under Sources).
- **Facts common to every return:** W-2 wages are the only income; no adjustments (Form 1040
  line 10 = 0); standard deduction; filer and spouse under 65 and not blind; nobody can claim the
  filer as a dependent; no Schedule 1-A deductions (2025: no qualified tips, overtime, car-loan
  interest or senior deduction); no QBI. Every child is a CTC-qualifying child with a valid SSN.
  There are no other dependents (Sch 8812 line 6 = 0, line 7 = 0), no Schedule 3 credits, no
  excess APTC, no AMT (checked below), and no Schedule 2 other taxes (Form 1040 line 23 = 0).

## Common worksheet facts

- **Credit Limit Worksheet A** (both years, Sch 8812 instructions p. 4): line 1 = Form 1040 line 18;
  line 2 = Schedule 3 lines 1, 2, 3, 4, 5b, 6d, 6f, 6l, 6m = 0; line 4 = 0 (CLW B not required
  because none of Forms 8396/8839/5695 Part I/8859 is claimed); line 5 = line 18. Form 1040
  line 18 = line 16 + line 17, and line 17 (Sch 2 line 3: AMT + excess APTC) = 0. So
  **Sch 8812 line 13 = Form 1040 line 16** in every scenario.
- **Line 3 (MAGI)** = line 1 (AGI) + line 2d (no Puerto Rico, Form 2555 or Form 4563 exclusions,
  so 0) = AGI.
- **Line 18a earned income** = W-2 wages (Earned Income Chart, Sch 8812 instructions p. 7 [2025],
  routing through the Form 1040 EIC Step 5 / Earned Income Worksheet; wages are the only earned
  income). Line 18b = 0.
- **AMT check (Form 6251)** for the two high-income scenarios. With wages only and the standard
  deduction, AMTI = taxable income + standard deduction (2024 Form 6251 lines 1 + 2a; 2025 lines
  1b + 2a, where 1a = Form 1040 line 14 − Sch 1-A line 37 = the standard deduction) = AGI.
  - 2024 MFJ: 420,000 − 133,300 = 286,700. That is above 232,600, so 286,700 × 0.28 − 4,652 =
    75,624, which is below the regular tax of 80,429. AMT = 0.
  - 2025 MFJ: 420,000 − 137,000 = 283,000. That is above 239,100, so 283,000 × 0.28 − 4,782 =
    74,458, which is below 78,934. AMT = 0.
  - 2024 Single: 215,500 − 85,700 = 129,800 × 0.26 = 33,748, below 41,975. AMT = 0.
  - 2025 Single: 215,500 − 88,100 = 127,400 × 0.26 = 33,124, below 40,983. AMT = 0.
  - The lower-income scenarios have AMTI at or below the exemption, so AMT = 0.

## Scenario 4: Single, wages $215,500, 1 child ($1,000 round-up rule)

dollars (50 cents rounds up) that is **41,975**.
Sch 8812: L3 = 215,500. L5 = 2,000 = L8. L9 = 200,000. L3 − L9 = 15,500, which is not a multiple of

form's rule gives 1,200, which is $25 less.

## Rounding rules applied

- **Tax Table:** the line 16 amount is read straight from the row containing taxable income, with
  rows half-open ("at least X but less than Y"). Whole dollars by construction.
- **Tax Computation Worksheet:** (a) × (b) − (d), in dollars and cents. Then I applied the 1040
  whole-dollar rounding rule ("You can round off cents to whole dollars... drop amounts under 50 cents
  and increase amounts from 50 to 99 cents to the next dollar... If you do round to whole dollars,
  you must round all amounts"). Only 2024 Scenario 4 produces cents ($41,974.50 → $41,975).
  Rounding is optional for the filer, so the unrounded value is valid too. A test should accept
  41,974.50 or 41,975 for 2024 S4 lines 16/18, and 40,774.50 or 40,775 for lines 22/24. A tolerance
  of $0.50 on those lines is justified by this rule. (Superseded for line 24 by the erratum
  below: Additional Medicare Tax applies, and the consistent results are enumerated.)
- **Sch 8812 line 10:** if positive and not a multiple of $1,000, round up to the next multiple of
  $1,000 (the form's own text). This is applied before the 5% on line 11.
- **Sch 8812 lines 11 and 20:** all products here come out as whole dollars, so no rounding was
  needed.

## Assumptions and ambiguities

1. **Age/blindness/dependency:** nobody is 65+ or blind and nobody can be claimed as a dependent,
   so the basic standard deduction applies. For 2025 this also means no senior deduction on
   Schedule 1-A.
2. **2025 Schedule 1-A:** wages contain no qualified tips or overtime premium, and there is no
   car-loan interest. Form 1040 line 13b = 0.
3. **Withholding and FICA (Scenario 5 Part II-B only):** for ordinary W-2 wages of $25,000, I took
   SS + Medicare withheld as 7.65% of wages (1,912.50), and the result does not depend on the
   exact value. The 7.65% rate is not stated in any document cited above. I used it only as an
   upper bound to show L25 < L20. Any FICA amount below $3,375 gives the same ACTC of $3,375. The
   instructions say EIC (line 27a) goes on Sch 8812 line 24, which only lowers L25, so the EIC was
   not computed.
4. **EIC:** Scenarios 2 and 5 (and possibly others) qualify for the EIC, which affects Form 1040
   payments but not lines 16–24 or the ACTC derived here. It was not derived.
5. **AMT:** checked on Form 6251 for Scenarios 3 and 4 (above); it is zero. No excess APTC, so
   Sch 2 line 3 = 0.
6. **2025 Form 1040 line numbering:** AGI is line 11a (repeated on 11b), and the standard deduction
   is line 12e, with line 14 = 12e + 13a + 13b. 2025 Sch 8812 line 1 reads from 1040 line 11a. The
   summary table's "L11" means 11a for 2025.
7. **Children 0 case:** Schedule 8812 is not filed, so L19 = 0 and L28 = 0.
8. **2025 Sch 8812 line 15** is "Reserved for future use". The 2024 line 15 is the opt-out checkbox
   for the ACTC, which I assumed was not checked.

## Erratum (2026-11-02, adjudicated after Codex blind spot-check; see ctc-2024-2025-review.md)

The L24 figures for scenarios 3 and 4 above are Form 1040 line 22, not line 24: they omit Additional Medicare Tax (Form 8959, via Schedule 2 line 11 -> 1040 line 23), which applies because Medicare wages exceed the threshold. 0.9% of wages over $250,000 (MFJ) / $200,000 (Single); same thresholds both years.

| # | Scenario | Year | L22 | Addl Medicare (8959) | L24 corrected |
|---|---|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 3 | MFJ $420k, 2 kids | 2024 | 77,429 | 170,000 x 0.009 = 1,530 | 78,959 |
| 3 | MFJ $420k, 2 kids | 2025 | 75,534 | 1,530 | 77,064 |
| 4 | Single $215.5k, 1 kid | 2024 | 40,774.50 (or 40,775 rounded) | 15,500 x 0.009 = 139.50 | 40,914 (40,915 if every line is rounded) |
| 4 | Single $215.5k, 1 kid | 2025 | 39,583 | 139.50 | 39,722.50 (39,723 if every line is rounded) |

Scenario 4 (both years): whole-dollar rounding is optional, but a return that rounds rounds every amount, so the two consistent line 24 results are the ones listed (cents kept; every line rounded). Tests enumerate both rather than using a tolerance band (superseding an earlier $0.50 band, per the independent review). Scenarios 1, 2, 5: L22 = L24 as stated (wages below the threshold).

Also noted: the official qualifying-child count is Schedule 8812 line 4 (lines 1-3 are AGI/modified AGI); line 6 is other dependents. For 2025, OBBBA additionally requires an SSN for the filer (at least one spouse if MFJ).
