# South Carolina SC1040 (full-year resident), tax years 2024 and 2025 — independent derivation

Derived 2026-11-11 by a blind Claude subagent from official SCDOR and IRS
publications only. Nothing under the tenforty repository was read except
`docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md`; no tenforty backend, graph, table or
other tax software was run. The only code used was a text lookup over the
official SC1040TT PDFs (row found by "at least / but less than").

**Read this first — tax year 2025 has a conformity fork.** South Carolina
conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as amended through 2024-12-31, so it did
not adopt the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) increase in the 2025 federal
standard deduction or the new $6,000 senior deduction. SC Information Letter
#26-4 (Revised) says taxpayers "will need to adjust their return" for those
items. The 2025 values below labelled **A (IL 26-4)** add those amounts back
and are the ones I believe are legally required. Values labelled **B (form as
printed)** make no add-back and are given only so a caller can tell which
treatment an implementation uses. See Uncertainties 1-3.

## Evidence kinds (README definitions)

- Every whole-return value here (federal taxable income, SC taxable income, SC
  tax) is an **Independent derivation**: the sources supply the rules, the
  deriver supplied the scenario and arithmetic. None is a published worked
  example.
- Each tax found in SC1040TT (line 5 under $100,000) rests on a **Published
  table row**, identified by year and interval in the arithmetic below. The SC
  table has a single column for all filing statuses.
- Taxes at line 5 of $100,000 or more are **Independent derivation** from the
  published Tax Rate Schedule formula (not a table row).
- Parameters ($4,790 / $4,930 exemption, $15,000 age-65 deduction, 44%, 6.2% /
  $659, 6% / $642, federal standard deduction amounts) are **published
  instruction amounts**, not whole-return examples.
- The only **Published worked examples** in the sources are the SC1040TT rate
  schedule examples (line 5 = $101,000: tax $5,603 for 2024, $5,418 for 2025)
  and the 44% example (net capital gain $2,000 -> deduction $880). They are
  not among the ten scenarios.

South Carolina:

- Rounding: "Round all amounts to the nearest whole dollar." (S24I and S25I,
  first sentence of "Line Instructions".) All entries below are whole dollars
  already except the two rate-schedule products, rounded as shown.
- Line 1: "Enter your taxable income from your federal form." (S24I/S25I, Line 1.)
- Line i: "Net capital gains included in taxable income are reduced by 44% ...
  Multiply the net capital gain by 44% and enter the result." (S24I/S25I, Line i.)
- Line q: "Beginning in the tax year a resident taxpayer reaches age 65, they
  are entitled to a deduction of $15,000 against any South Carolina income",
  reduced by retirement deductions on p-1/p-4; Example 1: no retirement income
  -> $15,000 on q-1. (S24I/S25I, Line q.) Same amount both years.
- Line t: deduction "for each dependent claimed on your federal Income Tax
  return who had not reached the age of 6 by December 31 of the tax year";
  worksheet amount $4,790 (2024, S24I) / $4,930 (2025, S25I) per such dependent.
- Line w: South Carolina dependent exemption "for each eligible dependent";
  worksheet amount $4,790 (2024) / $4,930 (2025) x number of dependents claimed
  on the federal return. A child under 6 gets both line t and line w.
- Line 5 = line 3 - line 4 (form, S24F/S25F).
- Line 6: "If your income subject to tax on line 5 is less than $100,000, use
  the SC1040TT ... Tax Tables ... If ... $100,000 or more, use the tax rate
  schedule provided in the SC1040TT." (S24I/S25I.) SC1040TT p. 4: "You must use
  the Tax Tables instead of this Tax Rate Schedule if your taxable income is
  less than $100,000."
- Rate schedule 2024 (S24T p. 4): line 5 x 6.2%, subtract $659.
- Rate schedule 2025 (S25T p. 4): line 5 x 6%, subtract $642.
- 2025 conformity (S25I "Before You Begin"): "South Carolina conforms to the
  Internal Revenue Code as amended through December 31, 2024, except as
  otherwise provided." S25I Line e, other additions: "Add back any federal
  deductions resulting from IRC sections that South Carolina does not adopt."
- IL26-4: "until the South Carolina Legislature addresses conformity, South
  Carolina taxpayers will need to adjust their return if the following 2025
  federal provisions are claimed on their federal return: 1. Standard
  Deduction. The standard deduction amounts for filing statuses have been
  increased by $750 for Single and Married Filing Separately, $1,125 for Head
  of Household, and $1,500 for Married Filing Jointly and Qualifying Surviving
  Spouse. 2. Senior Deduction. Individuals age 65 and older may claim an
  additional $6,000 deduction ..."

### Assumptions I supplied

- All wages are W-2 wages of the taxpayer; no adjustments to income, so federal
  AGI = total income. No qualified tips/overtime/car-loan-interest deductions.
- Every dependent is a qualifying child claimed on the federal return.
- Scenario 7 child and scenario 10 dependents: ages were not given; assumed
  6 or older on December 31 (no line t deduction). If any is under 6, subtract
  a further 4,790 (2024) / 4,930 (2025) per such child.
- Scenario 8: born before January 2, 1960 (2024) / 1961 (2025), not blind, no
  retirement income (lines p-1, p-4 zero), valid SSN.
- Scenario 9: the 10,000 is a net long-term capital gain with no short-term
  loss; taxed in federal taxable income in full.
- Scenario 4-6, 10: no Two Wage Earner Credit etc. — the requested figure is
  tax before credits anyway (line 10).
- 2025 variant A puts the IL 26-4 adjustment on line e (other additions); the
  letter itself does not name a line (Uncertainty 2).

## Per-scenario arithmetic — 2024

Federal taxable income (FTI) = wages (+ gains) - federal standard deduction (F24).

**1. Single, wages 40,000**
- FTI = 40,000 - 14,600 = 25,400 -> SC line 1 = 25,400
- Additions (line 2) = 0; line 3 = 25,400; subtractions (line 4) = 0
- Line 5 = 25,400 (< 100,000: tables required)
- S24T row 25,400-25,500 -> **$919**

**2. Single, wages 85,000**

## Uncertainties

1. **2025 conformity outcome (material: $48-$210 per scenario).** IL 26-4
   (2026-01-30) says the guidance holds "until the South Carolina Legislature
   addresses conformity" and promises updated guidance. What I could verify:
   the conformity bill H.3368 failed second reading in the Senate on
   2026-03-31 (via a summarised fetch of the legislature page; I could not get
   clean raw text of the history); the SCDOR FAQ fetched today still states
   conformity through December 31, 2024; IL 26-20 says Act 110 changes apply
   only to tax years after 2025; the SCDOR H.4216 notice (seen only in a search
   summary) says it does not affect 2025 returns. I found no later SCDOR letter
   superseding IL 26-4, but I cannot prove none exists, nor that no other bill
   conformed retroactively after March 2026. If conformity was enacted for
   2025, variant B is correct instead of A.
2. **Line placement of the 2025 adjustment.** IL 26-4 says to "adjust their
   return" without naming a line. I placed it on line e based on the S25I line
   e bullet about deductions from IRC sections South Carolina does not adopt.
   Line 5 and the tax are the same wherever it is entered, but a line-level
   fixture for line e/line 2 is my inference, not a quoted instruction. The
   2025 form and instructions (April/August 2025) predate IL 26-4 and contain
   no dedicated line or worksheet.
3. **Amount of the standard deduction add-back.** I used the increases stated
   in IL 26-4 ($750 / $1,125 / $1,500), not an independently fetched
   Rev. Proc. 2024-40. For scenario 8, I treated the add-back as 750 + 6,000
   and kept the 2,000 additional age-65 amount as allowed; IL 26-4 lists only
   the basic-amount increases and the senior deduction, which supports this,
   but it gives no worked example.
4. **An information letter "has no precedential value"** (its own scope
   statement). Variant A rests on S.C. conformity as described by SCDOR, not on
   a statute I read directly (S.C. Code 12-6-40 was not fetched).
5. **Dependent ages in scenarios 7 and 10** were unspecified; I assumed 6 or
   older. See the adjustment under Assumptions.
6. **Instruction page numbers** are not cited; passages are identified by line
   heading. The 2024 and 2025 IT packet PDFs could not be fetched (404 / 503);
   the stand-alone form, instructions and SC1040TT were used instead.
7. **Table transcription** was done by text extraction (pdftotext) of the
   official PDFs, not by visual reading of the rendered pages. Contiguity and
   two formula spot checks passed, but individual rows were not eyeballed
   against the rendered PDF.
8. The 2025 SC1040 form fetched is "Rev. 4/21/25" and SC1040TT "Revised
   6/17/25"; I did not find later revisions, but did not exhaustively check.
