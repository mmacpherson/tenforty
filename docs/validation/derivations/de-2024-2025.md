# Delaware PIT-RES 2024 and 2025: independent derivations

Derived 2026-11-11 by a blind Claude subagent from Delaware Division of Revenue
and IRS publications only. No tenforty code, spec, table, graph, test or other
fixture was read (only `docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md`, for the
evidence format). No tax software or calculator was run; the arithmetic below
was done by hand.

All figures are whole dollars. "DE tax" is Form PIT-RES **Line 33** (Balance:
Line 26 total tax less Line 32 non-refundable credits), with only the personal
credits on Lines 27a/27b claimed. It is before Line 34 (EITC), withholding and
every other credit.

Evidence kinds (README definitions):

- Every whole-return figure (Line 23, Line 33) is an **independent derivation**.
  The deriver supplied the scenario and arithmetic; none is a published worked
  example.
- Line 24 for scenarios 1, 2, 7, 8 and the 50,000 earner in 10b is a
  **published table row** inside an independent derivation.
- Line 24 for scenarios 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10a and the 70,000 earner in 10b is an
  **independent derivation** from the published tax schedule formula.
- The only **published worked example** in the sources is the schedule's own
  illustration (taxable income 67,751 gives 3,455.06, "Round to $3,455"). It is
  used here to establish the method and rounding, not as a scenario.

### Rules relied on, with locations

Page numbers are the printed page footers of the instructions. Wording is the
same in I24 and I25 unless noted.

1. **Federal AGI** (IRS24 lines 1a, 7, 9, 10, 11; IRS25 lines 1a, 7a, 9, 10,
   11a). Total income is wages plus capital gain; with no Schedule 1
   adjustments, AGI equals total income. PIT-RES Line 1 is "Federal AGI amount
   from federal Form 1040" (F24/F25 page 1). I24/I25 page 5, Line 1: filing
   statuses 1, 2 and 5 enter it in Column B.
2. **Delaware modifications** (F24/F25 page 1, Lines 2-11). Additions: Line 2
   non-Delaware state and local interest, Line 3 fiduciary adjustment and oil
   depletion. Subtractions: Line 5 U.S. obligation interest, Line 6 pension
   exclusion, Line 7 state refund and others, Line 8a Social Security and
   others, Line 8b 529/ABLE, Line 11 exclusion for persons 60 and over or
   disabled. None applies to wages or to a long-term capital gain; the form has
   no capital-gain modification line. The Line 11 worksheet (I24/I25 page 7)
   requires earned income under 2,500 and Line 10 of 10,000 or less for a
   single filer, so the age-67 filer with 50,000 of wages does not qualify.
3. **Standard deduction**, Line 20a (F24/F25 page 2; I24/I25 page 8 table):
   3,250 for filing statuses 1, 3 and 5; 6,500 for filing status 2; 3,250 in
   each of Columns A and B for filing status 4.
4. **Additional standard deduction**, Line 21 (F24/F25 page 2; I24/I25 page 8):
   2,500 per box checked for age 65 or over and for blind; standard deduction
   only.
5. **Taxable income**, Line 23: Line 12 less Line 22 (F24/F25 page 2).
6. **Tax**, Line 24 (I24/I25 page 8): "If Line 23 is less than $60,000, use the
   tax table to compute your tax liability. If line 23 is $60,000 or greater,
   use the tax schedule at the end of the tax table".
7. **Tax table** (T24/T25): rows are "At least / But less than / Tax due", 50
   wide from 2,000 upward, for taxable incomes under 60,000.
8. **Tax schedule** (T24/T25 page 3): "If taxable income on Line 23 of DE
   PIT-RES ... is $60,000 or [over] your tax is: $2,943.50 plus 6.60% (.066)
   ... for the portion over $60,000." Published example: taxable income 67,751;
   tax on 60,000 is 2,943.50; 7,751 x .066 = 511.56; total 3,455.06, "Round to
   $3,455."
9. **Personal credits**, Line 27a (F24/F25 page 2: "Enter number of exemptions
   x $110"; I24/I25 page 8). Example in the instructions: married filing
   jointly with no dependents enters 220 in Column B for filing status 2, or
   110 in each column for filing status 4. So each spouse and each dependent
   counts once at 110.
10. **Additional personal credit**, Line 27b (F24/F25 page 2; I24 page 8-9,
    I25 page 9): 110 for each of taxpayer and spouse aged 60 or over on
    December 31 of the tax year.
11. **Balance**, Line 33: Line 26 less Line 32, not below zero (F24/F25 page 2).
12. **Rounding** (I24/I25 page 4, "Rounding Off Dollars"): "Dollar amounts on
    your return must be rounded off to the nearest whole dollar. This means
    that amounts from 50 to 99 cents are increased to the next dollar. For
    example, $1.50 becomes $2.00 and $1.49 becomes $1.00."
13. **Filing status 4** (I24/I25 page 5): "you are in fact filing two separate
    returns which have been combined on the same form for convenience"; each
    spouse reports their own income, personal credits and deductions; both must
    use the same deduction method. Column A is the spouse, Column B the
    taxpayer (F24/F25 page 1). The instructions add: "Generally, separate
    returns (filing status 3 or 4) will be advantageous if both spouses have
    Delaware adjusted gross income in excess of $9,400."

## Per-scenario arithmetic

Line numbers are PIT-RES lines. Lines 2, 3, 5-8b, 11, 13-19 and 25 are zero in
every scenario, so Line 4 = Line 10 = Line 12 = Line 1 and Line 26 = Line 24.
The arithmetic is the same for 2024 and 2025. All amounts use Column B unless
stated.

### 1. Single, wages 20,000

- Federal AGI: 20,000 (Form 1040 line 1a 20,000; line 9 20,000; line 10 0)
- Line 1: 20,000. Delaware modifications: 0. Line 12 Delaware AGI: 20,000
- Line 20 standard deduction: 3,250. Line 21: 0. Line 22: 3,250
- Line 23: 20,000 - 3,250 = 16,750
- Line 24: under 60,000, tax table required. Row 16,750-16,800: 586
- Line 27a: 1 x 110 = 110. Line 27b: 0. Line 32: 110
- Line 33: 586 - 110 = **476**

### 2. Single, wages 40,000

- Federal AGI: 40,000. Line 12: 40,000
- Line 22: 3,250
- Line 23: 40,000 - 3,250 = 36,750
- Line 24: tax table required. Row 36,750-36,800: 1,655
- Line 27a: 110
- Line 33: 1,655 - 110 = **1,545**

### 3. Single, wages 85,000

- Federal AGI: 85,000. Line 12: 85,000
- Line 22: 3,250
- Line 23: 85,000 - 3,250 = 81,750
- Line 24: 60,000 or greater, tax schedule required
  - Excess over 60,000: 21,750
  - 21,750 x 0.066 = 1,435.50
  - 2,943.50 + 1,435.50 = 4,379.00, so 4,379
- Line 27a: 110
- Line 33: 4,379 - 110 = **4,269**

### 4. Single, wages 150,000

- Federal AGI: 150,000. Line 12: 150,000
- Line 22: 3,250
- Line 23: 150,000 - 3,250 = 146,750
- Line 24: tax schedule required
  - Excess: 86,750
  - 86,750 x 0.066 = 5,725.50
  - 2,943.50 + 5,725.50 = 8,669.00, so 8,669
- Line 27a: 110
- Line 33: 8,669 - 110 = **8,559**

### 5. Joint (filing status 2), wages 100,000, one earner, no dependents

- Federal AGI: 100,000. Line 12: 100,000
- Line 20: 6,500. Line 22: 6,500
- Line 23: 100,000 - 6,500 = 93,500
- Line 24: tax schedule required

## Uncertainties

1. **Retrieval.** `revenuefiles.delaware.gov` rejected a plain `curl` request
   ("Request Rejected"). The six Delaware PDFs were retrieved through the
   WebFetch tool, which saved the binaries; the text was then extracted locally
   with `pdftotext -layout`. Hashes are above. The guessed URL
   `https://revenuefiles.delaware.gov/2024/TY24_taxtable.pdf` returned 404; the
   correct 2024 table URL came from the Division's forms index (IDX).
2. **Table rows were read from extracted text, not from the rendered page.**
   The multi-column layout extracts with some interleaving. Each row used was
   matched as three consecutive tokens ("at least", "but less than", "tax
   due") and its neighbouring row was checked for monotonic sense, but nobody
   looked at the page image. A visual spot-check of the five rows would close
   this.
3. **Personal credit wording.** I24 and I25 Line 27a say "Enter the total
   number of dependents listed on your federal return, multiply by $110", which
   read literally would exclude the taxpayer. The form says "number of
   exemptions", and the instructions' own example gives 220 for a joint return
   with no dependents. This derivation counts each taxpayer, spouse and
   dependent once. That reading rests on the example and the form, not on the
   quoted sentence.
4. **Rate schedule below 60,000 is not printed.** The sources give only the
   table under 60,000 and the single formula at 60,000 and above. The lower
   brackets were neither needed nor verified here.
5. **Half-dollar rounding.** Scenarios 5, 6 and 10a land on exactly .50
   (5,154.50 and 6,474.50). The stated rule sends 50 cents up, giving 5,155 and
   6,475. The published example (3,455.06 to 3,455) confirms rounding to whole
   dollars but does not itself exercise the .50 case.
6. **Table against schedule at the boundary of a row.** The table is
   mandatory below 60,000, so table figures were used as printed. They may
   differ by a dollar from a formula evaluated at the exact taxable income,
   since each row covers a 50-dollar band.
7. **2025 federal AGI line.** I25 says to take AGI from "federal Form 1040,
   Line 11"; the 2025 Form 1040 labels it line 11a (and repeats it as 11b).
   The amount is unaffected.
8. **Credits outside scope.** Line 33 here excludes the Delaware EITC (Line
   34), child care credit (Line 31) and all others. Whether scenarios 1 or 7
   would have any federal EITC was not derived; the federal EITC tables were
   not fetched.
9. **2025 documents were revised in 2026** (form 2026-04-07, instructions
   2026-03-26). These are the versions retrieved; earlier revisions were not
   compared.
