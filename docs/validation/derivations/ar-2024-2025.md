- Deriver: blind Claude subagent, 2026-11-11. Official Arkansas DFA PDFs only.
- Not read: any tenforty spec, table, graph, test or fixture (only
  `docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md`, for the evidence format).
- Not run: tenforty, any tax software, any third-party calculator.
- All sources retrieved 2026-11-11.
- Federal inputs: none beyond the stipulated wages and (scenario 10) the
  stipulated federal net long-term capital gain. No IRS publication was needed.

## Evidence kinds used

- **Line 29 tax, net taxable income (NTI) under $100,001**: *published table
  row* (2024 / 2025 Regular Income Tax Table; row quoted per scenario).
- **Line 29 tax, NTI of $100,001 or more (scenario 5)**: *independent
  derivation* from the published rule printed on the table.
- **Every whole return (AGI, line 28, credits, line 38)**: *independent
  derivation*. The scenario and arithmetic are mine; the source supplies the
  rules. None of these is a published worked example.
- **Standard deduction, $29 credit, bracket adjustments**: published
  instruction / form / bracket-sheet amounts (parameters, not whole returns).

Read these three notes with the table:

1. **Scenario 4 is below the bracket-adjustment band in both years.** The band
   runs over NTI $92,301 to $95,500 for 2024 and $94,701 to $97,800 for 2025.
   Wages of 92,000 give NTI 89,590 / 89,530. The returns that actually land in
   the band are **scenarios 6 and 7 (both years)**, plus 4b (2024) and 4c (2025).
2. **Scenario 1 qualifies for a further $60 credit not included above.** The
   "Additional Tax Credit for Qualified Individuals" goes on AR1000TC line 6 and
   reaches the return through line 36 (other credits), which the task excludes.
   With it, line 38 would be 226 (2024) and 220 (2025).
3. **Scenario 9 takes three $29 credits ($87), not two.** The form has both a
   "65 or over" box and a "65 Special" box; a 67-year-old with wages only and no
   line 18 retirement exemption checks both.

### Bracket structure and the bracket adjustment (K24, K25)

The indexed bracket sheets give tax = rate x income minus a "Minus Adjustment",
evaluated "at the midpoint of each income level" and rounded; "the results must
match the table exactly".

| Year | Regular 3.9% bracket | Minus adjustment | Adjustment band (adjustment falls $10 per $100 of income) | Top |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | $25,700 to $92,300 | $409.86 | $92,301-$92,400: $397.40, then $387.40, ... $95,401-$95,500: $97.40. Asterisked row $94,301-$94,500 ($197.40) is $200 wide by design. | $95,501 and over: $87.40 |
| 2025 | $26,400 to $94,700 | $419.96 | $94,701-$94,800: $399.30, then $389.30, ... $97,701-$97,800: $99.30 | $97,801 and over: $89.30 |

Lower brackets used here: 2024 $15,600-$25,699 at 3.4% minus $281.37; 2025
$16,000-$26,399 at 3.4% minus $287.97.

Inside the band, each $100 of income adds $3.90 of rate tax and removes $10 of
adjustment, so table tax rises about $13.90 per $100 row instead of $3.90. The
published table rows already embody this; no separate worksheet is filed.

Each table row used below was also recomputed from the bracket sheet as a
cross-check ("K check"). Row midpoints: (lo + hi) / 2, e.g. row 82,501-82,601
has midpoint 82,551. All agree with the printed row.

## Uncertainties

1. **Scenario 4 does not exercise the bracket adjustment.** The brief expected
   wages of 92,000 to sit near the boundary. The published bands are on *net
   taxable income* ($92,301 for 2024, $94,701 for 2025), and NTI here is about
   89,500. The figures given are correct for the stated facts; the band is
   exercised by scenarios 6, 7, 4b (2024) and 4c (2025). If a band case for a
   single filer was the intent, 4b/4c are offered, but they are my additions.
2. **Scenario 5, over-$100,000 rule versus the bracket-sheet formula.** The
   booklet rule ($3,811 / $3,809 plus 3.9% of the excess over $100,000) gives
   5,667 / 5,663. Applying the bracket sheet's top-row formula directly (3.9% x
   NTI minus $87.40 / $89.30) would give 5,668.61 / 5,664.37, about $1.60 more.
   I used the booklet rule: it is the instruction printed on the table the form
   points to, and K25 repeats it. K24 as fetched does not print the
   over-$100,000 sentence; for 2024 the rule rests on B24 p. 30 and T24 alone.
   Nearest-dollar rounding of the result follows the form's general rule; the
   table note itself does not restate rounding.
3. **Scenario 1 additional credit.** The $60 Additional Tax Credit for
   Qualified Individuals is excluded because it is an AR1000TC "other credit"
   (line 36) and requires timely filing. A fixture that reports line 38 for a
   complete return should be 226 / 220, not 286 / 280.
4. **Scenario 9 credit count.** $87 assumes both the "65 or over" and "65
   Special" boxes, which is what the line 7A instruction gives for a wage-only
   67-year-old. The brief's phrase "the additional age-65 personal tax credit"
   (singular) may have anticipated one extra credit; the form gives two.
5. **Scenario 10 assumes** the 10,000 is federal Schedule D line 15 net
   long-term gain with no short-term activity and no depreciation adjustment.
   The AR1000D header sentence stating the 50% rule did not extract as clean
   text (font encoding); the 50% figure is taken from the cleanly extracted
   line 8, "multiply line 7b by 50 percent (.50)".
6. **Table boundary convention.** Rows are headed "As Much As / But Less
   Than"; below $74,900 they run X,X00 to X,X00, the row 74,900-75,001 is $101
   wide, and from 75,001 up they run X,X01 to X,X01. The bracket sheet words
   the same bands as "From ... Less Than or Equal To". These conventions
   disagree only for an NTI that is an exact multiple of $100 above $75,000.
   No scenario here is on such a boundary, so no value depends on it.
7. **Reading method.** Values were read from `pdftotext -layout` output of the
   official PDFs, not by eye from rendered pages. Mitigations: booklet and
   standalone tables agree on all 949 rows per year, and every row used
   reproduces from the separately published bracket sheet.
8. **Document revisions.** B25 carries table revision stamps of 09/29/2025
   (regular table) and 12/12/2025 (low income table), with PDF modification
   date 2025-12-17; B24's regular table is stamped 09/19/2024. A later DFA
   reissue would not be reflected.
