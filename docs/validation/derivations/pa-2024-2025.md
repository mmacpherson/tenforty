# Pennsylvania PA-40 independent derivations, tax years 2024 and 2025

Blind derivation, 2026-11-11. Sources: Pennsylvania Department of Revenue PDFs
only (listed below), fetched 2026-11-11. No tenforty code, spec, tables, graphs,
tests or other fixture files were read (only
`docs/validation/state-fixtures/README.md`, for the evidence format); no tax
software or calculator was run. Arithmetic is by hand.

Full-year PA residents, PA-40. "PA tax" below is PA-40 Line 12 less the Line 21
Tax Forgiveness Credit, i.e. tax before withholding, estimated payments, use tax
and other credits. Results are identical for 2024 and 2025 in every scenario:
the rate (3.07%), the Line 9 rule, the rounding rule and both Schedule SP
Eligibility Income Tables are the same in the two booklets.

### Rules relied on (same wording in I24 and I25 unless noted)

- **Rate, Line 12** — form Side 2, Line 12: "PA Tax Liability. Multiply Line 11
  by 3.07 percent (0.0307)." (F24, F25.) Instructions, "LINE 12. PA TAX
  LIABILITY": "Multiply Line 11 by 3.07 percent (0.0307)." (I24 p. 21, I25 p. 21.)
- **Line 9** — form Side 1, Line 9: "Total PA Taxable Income. Add only the
  positive income amounts from Lines 1c, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8. DO NOT ADD any
  losses reported on Lines 4, 5, or 6." Instructions p. 20 (both years): "Add
  only the positive income amounts from Lines 1c through 8. Do not add, subtract,
  or take losses into consideration. IMPORTANT: You cannot use a loss that you
  report in one income class on your tax return to reduce income in any other
  class."
- **Line 11** — "Adjusted PA Taxable Income. Subtract Line 10 from Line 9."
  Line 10 (Other Deductions, Schedule O) is zero in every scenario. I24 p. 20
  lists four deductions (MSA, HSA, IRC 529, IRC 529A ABLE); I25 says five, and
  I25 p. 1 "2025 State Tax Change" adds student loan interest up to $2,500. None
  is present in these scenarios.
- **No cross-class offset, no carryover** — p. 15 (both years): "Under PA law,
  losses may only be reported on Lines 4, 5, and 6. If entering a loss, fill in
  the oval next to the line. REMEMBER: You may not offset income in one PA income
  class with a loss in any other PA income class. You cannot carry forward or
  carry back gains or losses to other tax years."
- **Line 5** — p. 17 (both years): "Report your gain (loss) from the sale,
  exchange, or disposition of any kind of intangible property and any real or
  tangible property... IMPORTANT: PA law does not distinguish between long-term
  and short term gains (losses)..." and "If you realize an overall net loss, fill
  in the oval next to Line 5 on your PA-40." p. 18: "PA law recognizes a loss
  only on a transaction entered into for profit..."
- **Line 4** — p. 17 (both years): net income (loss) from an unincorporated
  business is reported on PA Schedule C; "Do not use federal Schedules C or F.
  Generally, you must adjust your federal amounts for PA tax purposes."
- **Rounding** — p. 4 (both years), "Rounding Numbers": "On the PA-40 form and
  schedules, show money amounts in whole-dollars. Eliminate any amount less than
  $0.50 and increase any amount that is $0.50 or more to the next highest
  dollar." The pre-signing checklist (I24 p. 25, I25 p. 25) repeats: "Round
  income, loss, deductions, and credit amounts to the nearest dollar".
- **Joint filing** — p. 15 (both years): "you and your spouse may file a joint
  tax return for convenience only. PA law does not provide any advantage when
  filing a joint return." There is no standard deduction, personal exemption or
  dependent exemption anywhere on PA-40 (Lines 1a–12, F24/F25); dependents matter
  only through Schedule SP.
- **Schedule SP** — form (SP24/SP25) Section III Line 1 "PA taxable income from
  Line 9 of your PA-40", Lines 2–10 other eligibility income, Line 11 total;
  Section IV Line 12 "PA Tax Liability from your PA-40, Line 12", Line 13 "Less
  Resident Credit", Line 14 net, Line 15 decimal from the table, Line 16 "Tax

## Stipulations common to all scenarios

- Full-year resident; all compensation is PA-taxable gross compensation (W-2);
  Line 1b unreimbursed employee business expenses = 0, so Line 1c = Line 1a.
- Line 10 = 0; Line 22 resident credit = 0; no Schedule OC/DC credits; no use tax.
- No eligibility income for Schedule SP beyond PA-40 Line 9 (SP Lines 2–10 = 0).
- Nobody is a dependent on another person's federal return.
- All inputs are whole dollars, so only Line 12 and Schedule SP Line 16 can need
  rounding.

Line numbers below are PA-40 lines. Lines 6, 7, 8 are 0 throughout and omitted.

## Per-scenario arithmetic (each applies identically to 2024 and 2025)

### 1. Single, compensation 40,000
- 1a 40,000; 1b 0; 1c 40,000. Lines 2, 3, 4, 5 = 0.
- L9 = 40,000. L10 = 0. L11 = 40,000.
- L12 = 40,000 × 0.0307 = 1,228.00 → **1,228**.
- Schedule SP: Table 1, 0 children; eligibility income 40,000 > 8,750 → not
  eligible; L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **1,228**.

### 2. Single, compensation 85,000
- 1c 85,000. L9 = L11 = 85,000.
- L12 = 85,000 × 0.0307 = 2,609.50 → "$0.50 or more to the next highest dollar"
  → **2,610**.
- Schedule SP: 85,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **2,610** (unrounded statutory product 2,609.50).

### 3. Single, compensation 150,000
- 1c 150,000. L9 = L11 = 150,000.
- L12 = 150,000 × 0.0307 = 4,605.00 → **4,605**.
- Schedule SP: 150,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **4,605**.

### 4. MFJ, compensation 100,000 (one earner), no dependents
- 1c 100,000. L9 = L11 = 100,000. No exemption or deduction for filing status.
- L12 = 100,000 × 0.0307 = 3,070.00 → **3,070**.
- Schedule SP: Table 2, "YOU & SPOUSE" row; 100,000 > 15,250 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **3,070**.

### 5. MFJ, compensation 100,000, two dependent children
- 1c 100,000. L9 = L11 = 100,000. PA-40 has no dependent exemption or credit on
  the income/tax lines.
- L12 = 3,070.00 → **3,070**.
- Schedule SP: Table 2, 2-children row; 100,000 > 34,250 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **3,070** (same as scenario 4).

### 6. Single, compensation 60,000 + 10,000 net gain on sale of long-term stock
- 1c 60,000. L5 = 10,000 (gain; PA does not distinguish long- from short-term,
  no preferential rate, no exclusion). Lines 2, 3, 4 = 0.
- L9 = 60,000 + 10,000 = 70,000. L11 = 70,000.
- L12 = 70,000 × 0.0307 = 2,149.00 → **2,149**.
- Schedule SP: 70,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **2,149**.
- Assumption: the 10,000 is the net gain as computed on PA Schedule D (PA basis
  equals the federal basis).

### 7. Single, compensation 60,000 + net business profit 20,000
- 1c 60,000. L4 = 20,000. Lines 2, 3, 5 = 0.
- L9 = 60,000 + 20,000 = 80,000. L11 = 80,000.
- L12 = 80,000 × 0.0307 = 2,456.00 → **2,456**.
- Schedule SP: 80,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **2,456**.
- Assumption: 20,000 is the net profit on **PA** Schedule C. PA-40 has no
  deduction for self-employment tax, self-employed health insurance or QBI
  (Line 10 is limited to the Schedule O items listed above), so nothing further
  reduces Line 4.

### 8. Single, compensation 60,000 + 3,000 interest + 2,000 dividends
- 1c 60,000. L2 = 3,000. L3 = 2,000. Lines 4, 5 = 0.
- L9 = 60,000 + 3,000 + 2,000 = 65,000. L11 = 65,000.
- L12 = 65,000 × 0.0307 = 1,995.50 → next highest dollar → **1,996**.
- Schedule SP: 65,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **1,996** (unrounded statutory product 1,995.50).
- Assumption: the interest is PA-taxable (not from U.S. or Pennsylvania
  obligations) and the dividends are PA-taxable ordinary dividends. Qualified
  status is irrelevant: one flat rate.

### 9. MFJ, compensation 20,000, two dependent children
- 1c 20,000. L9 = L11 = 20,000.
- L12 = 20,000 × 0.0307 = 614.00 → **614**.
- Schedule SP (Section I oval 3a, married claiming together; Column A; Table 2):
  - Section II Line 2: 2 dependent children → PA-40 Line 19b = 2.
  - Section III Line 1 = 20,000 (PA-40 Line 9); Lines 2–10 = 0; Line 11 = 20,000
    → PA-40 Line 20 = 20,000.
  - Table 2, 2-children row: 20,000 does not exceed 32,000 → 100%, decimal 1.0.
  - Line 12 = 614; Line 13 = 0; Line 14 = 614; Line 15 = 1.0;
    Line 16 = 614 × 1.0 = **614** → PA-40 Line 21 = 614.
- **Forgiveness applies in full: 100%, $614.** PA tax = 614 − 614 = **0**.
- Note on form mechanics: Line 12 stays 614. The forgiveness is a credit on
  Line 21 in the payments-and-credits block (Line 24), not a reduction of
  Line 12. Any PA withholding would be refunded in full.
- Margin: the couple would stay at 100% up to 32,000 of eligibility income and
  lose all forgiveness above 34,250.

### 10. Single, compensation 60,000 and a 5,000 net LOSS on sale of property
- 1c 60,000. L5 = 5,000 with the LOSS oval filled in (a net loss in the
  sale-of-property class). Lines 2, 3, 4 = 0.
- L9: "Add only the positive income amounts... DO NOT ADD any losses reported on
  Lines 4, 5, or 6" → L9 = 60,000. L11 = 60,000.
- L12 = 60,000 × 0.0307 = 1,842.00 → **1,842**.
- Schedule SP: Line 1 is PA-40 Line 9 = 60,000 > 8,750 → L21 = 0.
- PA tax = **1,842**.
- Treatment of the loss: it is reported on Line 5 but has no effect on tax. A
  loss in one PA income class cannot offset income in any other class
  (compensation here), and it cannot be carried forward or back. The class
  contributes 0 to Line 9, not −5,000. There is no $3,000 allowance as in federal
  law. Assumes the property was held for profit, so the loss is recognised at all.

## Uncertainties

1. **Half-dollar rounding of Line 12 (scenarios 2 and 8).** The general rule on
   p. 4 covers all "money amounts" on the form and rounds $0.50 up, giving 2,610
   and 1,996. The pre-signing checklist names only "income, loss, deductions,
   and credit amounts", not tax, and the Line 12 instruction itself says nothing
   about rounding. I read the p. 4 rule as governing Line 12 (the form has no
   cents field that I could confirm from text extraction — not verified
   visually). If an implementation keeps cents, the exact products are 2,609.50
   and 1,995.50. All other scenarios produce whole-dollar products and are
   unaffected.
2. **"PA tax" definition for scenario 9.** The form's Line 12 "PA Tax
   Liability" is 614; the net-of-forgiveness figure 0 is my L12 − L21, not a
   printed line. A fixture must compare against whichever of the two the
   implementation's output represents.
3. **Stipulated PA-level amounts (scenarios 6, 7, 8, 10).** The gain, loss,
   business profit, interest and dividends are taken as already-PA-computed
   class amounts. I did not derive PA Schedule C/D/A/B adjustments from federal
   figures; a real return's PA Schedule C profit commonly differs from the
   federal one.
4. **Statute not consulted.** The 3.07% rate and the Schedule SP tables are
   taken from the Department of Revenue forms and booklets, not from 72 P.S.
   § 7302 / § 7304 directly.
5. **Text extraction.** Tables were read from `pdftotext -layout` output, not
   viewed as rendered pages. The Table 1 and Table 2 rows used were consistent
   between the booklet and the Schedule SP instruction copy and between years
   (each row steps by 250 across columns and 9,500 per child), so a
   transcription error is unlikely but the pages were not checked by eye.
6. **Page numbers** are PDF page indices of the booklet; they matched the
   printed "PA-40 nn" footers where I checked (pp. 13–39).
7. No source failed to download; nothing in this record is guessed.
