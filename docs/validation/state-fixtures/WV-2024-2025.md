- Deriver: blind Claude subagent, 2026-11-11, West Virginia Tax Division IT-140

- Pinned quantities: `state_taxable_income` is IT-140 line 7 and
  `state_total_tax` is the graph's line 12 (line 8 less the Schedule FTC

  children). The graph applies no default personal exemption.
- Checked mechanically against the record's summary table.

### Permitted alternatives

None. The Tax Table row is mandatory below $100,000 and Rate Schedule I above
(record, Uncertainty 4), so each case has one outcome.

### Not pinned

Scenario 10 (Single age 67, wages 50,000; both years): the Schedule M senior
citizen deduction of 8,000 is a line 3 subtraction, and no public input
reaches the graph's `wv_subtractions`.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Taxable income matches the record in all 20 cases. Tax departs in all 20:

- `tenforty-tj2.20`: the Tax Table is computed at the row midpoint, and the
  form requires the row below $100,000. The graph prices Rate Schedule I on the
  exact income. Bound: marginal rate of the bracket times
  (taxable income minus row midpoint), plus or minus $0.50 for the table's
  whole-dollar rounding. The rows are the record's (for example 40,000 to
  40,050, midpoint 40,025). Applied to the 18 cases priced from a table row.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: Single 150,000 (Rate Schedule I, over $100,000) is rounded
  to the whole dollar by the form; the graph keeps the cents. Bound plus or
  minus $0.50. Applied to the two scenario 6 cases.
- Related, open and not signed here: `tenforty-s48h.48` (no default personal
  exemption, which is why exemptions are passed explicitly) and
  `tenforty-s48h.80` (an unexplained kink at 40,000 wages).

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
each defect's mechanism and the record's own rows and rates, never from the
observed residual.
