## Exemption/credit repair (2026-11-04, tenforty-b72.53)

The archived derivation above is unchanged. Its values drive both the official
fixture tests and the raw component tests. No tax amount or tolerance was tuned
to graph output. The 32 original OH strict signatures all reported matches
before retirement. Exemption-tier, filing-status, $650 cap and eligibility
controls apply the archive's published rules; they are not a new blind
whole-return derivation.

The graph now computes the personal/spouse base plus total dependents, the
MAGI-tiered exemption amount (including the 2025 $750,000 cutoff), the $20
exemption credit, and whole-dollar tax entries. Both years use the published
$2,394.32 upper-bracket base; the 2025 discontinuity at $100,000 is retained.
The bracket-rate output remains based on taxable nonbusiness income.

Raw graph controls `oh_it1040_FilerQualifyingIncome` and
`oh_it1040_SpouseQualifyingIncome` are each spouse's qualifying Ohio income,
excluding interest, dividends/distributions, capital gains, rents and royalties.
Both must reach $500 for the MFJ joint credit. Raw
`oh_it1040_PreJointCredits` supplies the aggregate of other Schedule of Credits
lines 2-8, before the exemption and joint credits; do not also enter those same
credits through the legacy individual-credit controls. The archived cases have
all other credits zero.

The supported source cases are full-year nonbusiness returns, not claimable as
someone else's dependent, with the other assumptions in the archive. Natural
business-income adjustments and claimable-filer exemptions are not added here.
`dependent_exemptions` remains unmapped for OH; use total `num_dependents`.

**Pending policy:** natural MFJ joint-credit behavior has not been changed.
`_ohio_natural_joint_credit_values` is the single policy switch point and leaves
both raw spouse controls unset pending Mike's convention decision (SOL-18).
OH remains excluded from automatic graph scope under b72.53 during this interim
state; that bead is unsigned after the corrected value signatures retire.
