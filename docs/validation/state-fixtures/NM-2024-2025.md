### Permitted alternatives

None. Each case has one outcome: the sources fix the line 18 method (table at
or below $100,000, page T-7 above) and the whole-dollar rule (50 cents rounds
up, PIT-1 instructions p. PIT-1-21). The record's open questions (2025 Schedule
1-A senior deduction in scenario 8; a business-sale gain in scenario 9) are
settled by the cited line references, and the record pins one reading of each.

### Not pinned

None. Credits and rebates (PIT-RC, working families credit) are outside line
22 and were never part of the record's tax figure.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Every one of the 20 cases departs; none matches exactly. Taxable income
matches in 12 cases (2024 and 2025 scenarios 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8) and departs in
8 (scenarios 1, 6, 9 and 10, both years).

- `tenforty-tj2.20`: the form requires the Tax Rate Table row ($100 bands)
  at or below $100,000, and the graph prices the bracket schedule at the exact
  income, in cents. The bound is the half-row width at the top rate plus the
  whole-dollar rounding, 0.049 x 100 / 2 + 0.50 = 2.95. Above $100,000 (page
  T-7, scenarios 4 and 10) the schedule's base is the last table row's tax,
  not the schedule's tax at $100,000, so one more rounding step is allowed:
  3.45. Typical size: scenario 2, 2024, graph 965.10 against 963.
- `tenforty-s48h.62`: the graph leaves PIT-1 line 13 (deduction for certain
  dependents, $4,000 times dependents minus one) and line 15 (PIT-ADJ net
  capital gains deduction: 2024 greater of $1,000 or 40% of the gain, 2025
  $2,500) at zero, because `num_dependents` and long-term gains do not feed
  them. Taxable income is high by exactly the record's omitted amount
  (scenario 6 and 10: 4,000 both years; scenario 9: 4,000 in 2024, 2,500 in
  2025). The tax bound is the omitted amount times the graph's marginal rate
  over the omitted span (0.049 in 2024; 0.047 in 2025 for scenarios 6 and 9,
  0.049 for scenario 10), plus the `tenforty-tj2.20` slack. The record gives
  the same figure for scenario 6 independently: scenario 5 is the same return
  without the deduction, and 3,059 - 2,863 = 196 (2024), 2,606 - 2,418 = 188
  (2025). Typical size: scenario 6, 2024, taxable income +4,000, tax about +198.
- `tenforty-b72.77` (no bead yet): the graph's
  line 14 (low- and middle-income exemption) is an input, not computed from
  AGI, filing status and exemptions as the worksheet on page PIT-1-26 does.
  Scenario 1 (AGI $20,000, Single) is eligible for $2,500; the graph leaves it
  at zero. Taxable income is high by 2,500; tax by 2,500 times the lowest
  bracket rate (0.017 in 2024, 0.015 in 2025), plus the table slack. Typical
  size: 2024, taxable income 5,400 against 2,900.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the observed
residual.
