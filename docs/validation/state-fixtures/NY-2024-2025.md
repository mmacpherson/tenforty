| Tests | Evidence kind | Notes |

| `tests/state_official_values_test.py`, NY cases | Independent derivation | 18 returns (9 per year, identical figures in both years): Single wage returns at $40,000, $85,000 and $150,000; MFJ $100,000 with and without two dependents; HoH $60,000 with one dependent; Single age 67; Single wages plus a long-term gain; MFJ $250,000 with two dependents. ### Permitted alternatives

None. IT-201-I p. 2 fixes the rounding ("Drop amounts that are less than 50
cents ... Increase amounts that are 50 cents or more to the next dollar"), so
the one exact half in the set (scenario 2, $4,070.50) has the single value
$4,071. The line 39 method is fixed by line 33 and line 38 (IT-201-I p. 12 and
p. 32), so the table and the schedule are never both legal. The record's open
question on rounding worksheet intermediate lines changes no figure here
(scenario 3 gives $8,433 either way; scenario 9 has no fractional amounts).

### Not pinned

None. The Empire State child credit (line 63) is a refundable credit after
line 46 and does not enter either pinned quantity.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

No return matches: all 18 are signed. Taxable income matches exactly in the
twelve returns without dependents.

- `tenforty-b72.49`: `num_dependents` does not reach line 36
  (`NYIT201_2024.hs`/`NYIT201_2025.hs` line 64, a `keyInput` no natural input
  maps to). Taxable income is high by exactly $1,000 per dependent (signature
  exact); tax is high by that amount times the bracket rate the record's
  schedule gives (5.5%, or 6% for MFJ $250,000). Cases: MFJ100dep2, HoH60dep1,
  MFJ250dep2.
- `tenforty-b72.4`: line 39 is `bracketTax` on the rate schedule alone (spec
  line 75-77); the tax computation worksheets' recapture is absent. The bound
  is the record's own worksheet figure: worksheet 7 line 8, $481.096, for
  Single $150,000; worksheet 2 lines 4 and 9, $333 + $807 = $1,140, for MFJ
  $250,000. Cases: S150, MFJ250dep2.
- `tenforty-tj2.20`: below $65,000 with NY AGI at or under $107,650 the tax
  table is required; the graph prices the schedule at the taxable income. Each
  of the record's rows is $50 wide and the return sits on the row's lower
  edge, so the graph is under the row's midpoint figure by 5.5% × $25 =
  $1.375. Cases: S40, S50age67, S60gain, and the table part of HoH60dep1.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents where line 39 is a whole-dollar
  entry, and it accumulates the lower brackets with their cents where the
  schedule (IT-201-I p. 33) prints a whole-dollar base. The base gap is
  computed from the record's transcribed schedule, rate × width of each lower
  bracket less the printed base: Single 5.5% bracket −$0.50 ($599.50 against
  $600), Single 6% −$0.25, MFJ 5.5% $0.00, MFJ 6% −$0.25, HoH 5.5% −$0.125.
  Cases: S85 and MFJ100 alone; it is a component of every other bound.

Each tax bound is the sum of the mechanisms that apply to the return, ± $0.50
for the whole-dollar rule (plus the runner's half-cent comparison tolerance,
because an exact half is a real outcome of the 50-cents-up rule):

| Case | Centre of the tax bound | Components |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| S40, S50age67, S60gain | −1.875 | −1.375 table half row, −0.50 base gap |
| S85 | −0.50 | base gap |
| MFJ100 | 0.00 | cents only |
| S150 | −481.346 | −481.096 worksheet 7 line 8, −0.25 base gap |
| MFJ100dep2 | +110.00 | 5.5% × $2,000 |
| HoH60dep1 | +53.50 | 5.5% × ($1,000 − $25), −0.125 base gap |
| MFJ250dep2 | −1,020.25 | −$1,140 recapture, +6% × $2,000, −0.25 base gap |

A signature carries one bead; where two mechanisms meet in one return the
signature names the larger and its reason names the other. When either is
repaired the return leaves its bound and the signature must be rederived.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the observed
residual. `tenforty.graph_scope` already excludes NY in both years under
`tenforty-b72.4` and `tenforty-tj2.20` (and `tenforty-nuc6`); it does not yet
name `tenforty-b72.49` or `tenforty-b72.42`.
