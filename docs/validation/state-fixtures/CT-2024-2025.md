### Permitted alternatives

For Connecticut AGI up to $102,000 the Line 6 instructions say the filer "may"
use the Tax Tables; they do not bar the schedule (record, Uncertainties 1).
Each of those seven returns therefore lists two whole outcomes, the table row
first and the exact-AGI schedule figure second, which differ by $1. Scenarios 3
and 9 are above $102,000, where the schedule is the only paper method, so each
has one outcome. The schedule's half-up rounding is inferred from the Table B
examples (Uncertainties 2); it matters only to scenario 1's schedule figure,
which is 1,192 under per-line rounding. The unrounded 1,192.50 is not a
permitted outcome: Line 6 is a whole-dollar entry under the CT-1040
instructions, so a figure in cents cannot be the return's tax.

### Not pinned

None. Dependents and age are accepted inputs but do not enter Line 6 (record,
Connecticut AGI), so scenarios 4 and 5 share one figure.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- `state_taxable_income` is schedule line 3 (`L3_ct_taxable_income`, AGI less
  the phased-out Table A exemption). It matches the record in all 18 cases.
- `state_total_tax` is `L18_ct_total_tax`: Table B initial tax (schedule line 4)
  plus the `ct_phaseout_addback` and `ct_tax_recapture` key inputs (schedule
  lines 5 and 6, left at zero by the public inputs), less credit inputs. The
  Table E personal tax credit (schedule line 9) has no node. The graph tax is
  therefore the record's schedule line 4, and it differs from Line 6 in every
  case.
- `tenforty-b72.62`: the 2% add-back (Table C), tax recapture (Table D) and personal tax
  credit (Table E) are not computed, and the Tax Tables row (a midpoint-priced
  figure, `tenforty-tj2.20`'s mechanism) is not used. The signature measures
  the record's schedule line 4 less the primary (table) outcome, ± $0.005
  (revised 2026-11-25 after review); the schedule outcome is accepted only as
  an exact match. Examples from the record's own lines: Single $40,000, line 4
  of 1,325 less the table's 1,191 (graph over by exactly 134); Single $85,000,
  add-back 150 (graph under by exactly 149 against the table); Single $150,000, add-back 250 and recapture 225
  (graph under by exactly 475); MFJ $400,000, add-back 500 and recapture 500
  (graph under by exactly 1,000).

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it matches any permitted outcome. Bounds come from the
record's schedule lines, never from the observed residual.
