### Rule summary (with citations)

1. **Starting point.** IT-40 line 1 = federal AGI from Form 1040/1040-SR line 11 (F24, F25 line 1).
   Line 3 = line 1 + line 2 (add-backs, Schedule 1); line 5 = line 3 − line 4 (deductions,
   Schedule 2). In this domain lines 2 and 4 are blank, so line 5 = federal AGI.
2. **Exemptions (line 6 = Schedule 3 line 7).** Identical structure both years (S3-24, S3-25; B24 pp. 23–25, B25 pp. 23–25):
   - Sch 3 line 1: **$2,000 if MFJ; otherwise $1,000.** B24/B25 p. 23 "Line 1 – Exemptions for taxpayer, spouse (if married filing jointly)": "All other filers should enter $1,000 on this line … even if you are claimed on someone else's tax return."
   - Sch 3 line 2: number of dependents (Sch IN-DEP Box 5) × **$1,000**.
   - Sch 3 line 3: additional exemption for each qualifying dependent child (son/stepson/daughter/stepdaughter/foster child/legal-guardian child; under 19 at Dec 31, or full-time student under 24; and claimable on line 2) — Sch IN-DEP Box 6 × **$1,500**. Box 6 = count of Xs in boxes nE **and** nF. Box F is checked when the child is claimed for the additional exemption *for the first time* (B24/B25 p. 25: "permitted to claim an amount of $3,000 instead of $1,500 for one tax year"; not permitted if the child could have been claimed for the $1,500 in any previous year). So a first-year child yields 2 × $1,500 = $3,000.
   - Sch 3 line 4: each of you/spouse age 65+ and/or blind × **$1,000** (B p. 24).
   - Sch 3 line 5: additional **$500** for each 65+ taxpayer/spouse if IT-40 line 1 (federal AGI) < $40,000 (< $20,000 if MFS) (B p. 24).
   - Sch 3 line 6: adopted-child additional × **$3,000** (Sch IN-DEP-A).
   - Sch 3 line 7: sum of lines 1–6 → IT-40 line 6.
3. **Indiana AGI.** IT-40 line 7 = line 5 − line 6 (F24, F25).
4. **State AGI tax (line 8).**
   - 2024: "State adjusted gross income tax: multiply line 7 by **3.05% (.0305)** (if answer is less than zero, leave blank)" (F24 line 8).
   - 2025: "… multiply line 7 by **3% (.03)** …" (F25 line 8).
   - Corroborated by B24 p. 37 (Group A worksheet ".0305") and B25 p. 37 ("3% (.03)").
5. **Rounding.** "Round all entries" on the form; B24 p. 5 / B25 p. 4–5 "Rounding Required": round to the nearest whole dollar, drop < $0.50, raise $0.50 or more (example: $432.50 → $433). Line 8 is therefore rounded half-up to whole dollars.
6. **County tax (line 9)** comes from Schedule CT-40 and depends on county of residence/work on Jan 1 (B25 p. 7 "Line 9 – County Tax"). Not computed here.
7. **Filing status.** Indiana has no rate schedule by status; the only status-dependent items in this domain are Sch 3 line 1 ($2,000 MFJ vs $1,000 everyone else) and the line 5 65+ income threshold. Federal MFJ ⇒ Indiana MFJ; federal MFS ⇒ Indiana MFS (B24 p. 4, B25 p. 4 "Married Filing Requirements"). There is no Indiana HoH or QSS box; HoH and QSS filers are "all other filers" ($1,000 on Sch 3 line 1).

### Arithmetic

2024 rate .0305 (F24 line 8); 2025 rate .03 (F25 line 8); round half-up to whole dollar (B "Rounding Required").

1. 100,000 − 1,000 = 99,000; 99,000 × .0305 = 3,019.50 → 3,020 (exactly .50 rounds up).
2. 50,000 − 1,000 = 49,000; × .0305 = 1,494.50 → 1,495.
3. Ex = 2,000 + 2×1,000 + 2×1,500 = 7,000; 90,000 − 7,000 = 83,000; × .0305 = 2,531.50 → 2,532.
4. Ex = 1,000 + 1,000 + 1,500 = 3,500; 45,000 − 3,500 = 41,500; × .0305 = 1,265.75 → 1,266.
5. 40,000 − 1,000 = 39,000; × .0305 = 1,189.50 → 1,190.
6. Ex = 3,500; 70,000 − 3,500 = 66,500; × .0305 = 2,028.25 → 2,028.
7. 20,000 − 1,000 = 19,000; × .0305 = 579.50 → 580.
8. 99,000 × .03 = 2,970.
9. 49,000 × .03 = 1,470.
10. 83,000 × .03 = 2,490.
11. 41,500 × .03 = 1,245.
12. 39,000 × .03 = 1,170.
13. 66,500 × .03 = 1,995.
14. 19,000 × .03 = 570.

Note: five of the 2024 cases land exactly on $.50; they are sensitive to the half-up rule
(banker's rounding would give 3,020 / 1,494 / 2,532 / 1,190 / 580 — only case 2 differs, 1,494 vs 1,495).
A tolerance of $0 is justified by the published whole-dollar rounding rule; a ±$1 tolerance would
be needed only if an implementation does not round line 8.

### Ambiguities / open questions

1. **Statute not retrieved.** IC 6-3-2-1 (rates) and IC 6-3-1-3.5 (exemptions) could not be fetched (justia 403/Cloudflare; iga.in.gov JS-only). A secondary search snippet quoting IC 6-3-2-1 reports 3.05% for taxable years beginning after Dec 31, 2023 and before Jan 1, 2025, and 3% for after Dec 31, 2024 and before Jan 1, 2026 — consistent with F24/F25 line 8, but the form is the cited authority here.
2. **First-year dependent child ($3,000).** Sch IN-DEP Box F doubles the additional exemption the first year a child is eligible. The scenario spec ("qualifying children under 19") does not say whether the child is newly eligible (e.g., born in the tax year). The main table assumes **not** first-year; variant rows 4F/11F show the effect. An implementation that ignores Box F will under-exempt newborns.
3. **MFS spouse exemption.** Indiana Sch 3 line 1 gives MFS filers $1,000 only, with no allowance for a non-filing spouse (unlike some states). Source text is unambiguous ("All other filers"), so MFS = $1,000.
4. **HoH/QSS.** No distinct Indiana status; treated as "all other filers" ($1,000). Married-living-apart filers who filed federal HoH do not check the IN MFS box (B p. 4).
5. **Line 8 rounding.** The form says "Round all entries"; the booklet rounding rule is half-up. I did not find a line-8-specific instruction beyond the form text; the five exact-.50 cases in 2024 depend on this.
6. **Negative line 7.** Line 8 instruction: "if answer is less than zero, leave blank" → tax floor at $0 (not exercised by these scenarios).
7. **County tax** (line 9, Schedule CT-40) depends on county of residence/principal employment on Jan 1 and is excluded; total Indiana tax (line 11) therefore is not derived here.
