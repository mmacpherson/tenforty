### Which lines are pinned

The graph reports Form 740 **line 11** as `state_taxable_income` and **line 12**
(line 11 × .04) as `state_total_tax` (`src/tenforty/mappings.py`, KY outputs
`L11_ky_taxable_income` and `L12_ky_tax`). The pinned values are therefore the
record's rows "11 Taxable income" and "12 whole dollars".

The record's summary "State tax" column is **line 22**, tax after the personal
tax credits (line 17) and the family size tax credit (line 21). It is not what
`state_total_tax` reports and is not pinned. Lines 12 and 22 are equal in
twelve of the eighteen returns and differ in six:

| Scenario | Year | Line 12 (pinned) | Line 22 (record's state tax) | Why they differ |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| s1 Single, wages 15,000 | 2024 | 474 | 0 | 100% family size tax credit |
| s1 | 2025 | 469 | 0 | 100% family size tax credit |
| s7 Joint, wages 30,000, two children | 2024 | 1,074 | 0 | 100% family size tax credit |
| s7 | 2025 | 1,069 | 0 | 100% family size tax credit |
| s8 Single, age 67, wages 50,000 | 2024 | 1,874 | 1,834 | $40 age-65 personal tax credit |
| s8 | 2025 | 1,869 | 1,829 | $40 age-65 personal tax credit |

So the fixtures confirm the graph's line 12 for these six returns and say
nothing about the tax the filer owes. See "Not covered" below.

### Permitted alternatives

Kentucky prints "00" in the cents position of every money line, so line 12 is a
whole-dollar entry, but the packets, forms, schedules and KY-1345 e-file
handbooks state no rounding rule (record, Uncertainties 1). Nearest dollar is
the primary outcome; dropped cents is also accepted where it differs, because
the cited instructions leave the rounding convention unstated.

- 2024: every line 12 product ends in .60, so each of the nine returns permits
  the nearest dollar and $1 lower (474 or 473, 1,474 or 1,473, and so on).
- 2025: every product ends in .20, so both readings give the same dollar and
  there is one outcome.

Each alternative is a whole (taxable income, tax) outcome with the same line 11.

### Not pinned

None. Every record scenario is expressible: `w2_income`,
`long_term_capital_gains`, `num_dependents=2` for the two children (s6, s7) and
`filer_65_or_older` (s8).

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- Line 11 matches exactly in all 18 returns.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph's line 12 is `l11 .*. rate` with no rounding
  (`tenforty-spec/forms/KYForm740_2024.hs` and `KYForm740_2025.hs`, lines
  54-56), so it keeps the cents where the form takes whole dollars. Signed on
  all 18 returns, because every pinned product has cents. Bound ± $0.50 against
  the nearest-dollar outcome: the distance from any amount to its nearest
  dollar is at most half a dollar. The graph returns the record's "Line 12
  exact" figure in every case.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in the module's `DEFECTS`: the
test fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.

### Not covered: the credits between line 12 and line 22

These are graph gaps the line 12 pin cannot see. No signature records them,
because no checked quantity carries them.

- **Family size tax credit** (Schedule ITC Section C, Form 740 lines 20-21).
  The spec takes it as a raw input (`L13 family_size_credit`) that no public
  input reaches and does not compute it from family size and modified gross
  income. For s1 and s7 the filer owes $0 and `state_total_tax` reports $474 /
  $469 and $1,074 / $1,069.
- **Age-65 personal tax credit** (Schedule ITC Section B, Form 740 line 17),
  `tenforty-s48h.56`: `filer_65_or_older` does not change any KY output. For s8
  line 22 is $40 below line 12.
- The spec's line numbers after line 12 do not follow the 2024 or 2025 Form 740
  (its L13 is the family size credit and its L19 is "total tax"; on the form
  those are lines 21 and 26).

KY stays out of graph scope (`tenforty.graph_scope`) while these are open.
