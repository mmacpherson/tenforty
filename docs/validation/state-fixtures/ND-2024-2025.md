### Permitted alternatives

Record U1: neither ND booklet nor form states a rounding rule for rate-schedule
tax (2024 and 2025 Single at 150,000 and 300,000). Each of those four returns
lists two whole outcomes: the schedule's cents, half-up (primary), and the
federal whole-dollar convention. Tax Table returns are whole dollars as printed,
so each has one outcome.

### Not pinned

None. Record U2 (2025 table has no row below 40,250) and U3 (2025 age-67 return
assumes the Schedule 1-A senior deduction) do not change a pinned value; tax is 0
in all three cases.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- `tenforty-tj2.20`: for Single wages 85,000 (both years) the form requires the
  Tax Table row, a midpoint price rounded to the dollar, and the graph prices
  the schedule at the income. Each such return sits on its $50 row's lower
  edge, so the graph is rate x $25 (1.95% x 25 = $0.4875) under the midpoint
  figure, plus the table's own whole-dollar rounding (within $0.50). Bound
  -0.4875 +/- 0.505.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: Single 150,000 (2024) and 300,000 (2025). The graph keeps
  the schedule's sub-cent fraction where the schedule prints cents and the form
  states no rounding rule; the graph sits within a half cent to a cent of the
  cent outcome and within $0.50 of the whole-dollar one. Bound +/- 0.505.
  The other two schedule returns agree within the runner tolerance and carry no
  signature.
- `tenforty-b72.73`: ND-1 line 1b is federal taxable
  income (Form 1040 line 15), so it carries the age-65 additional standard
  deduction and, in 2025, the Schedule 1-A Part V senior deduction. The graph
  subtracts its own standard deduction from federal AGI and applies neither
  (`NDForm1_2024.hs` and `NDForm1_2025.hs`, line L4). Taxable income is high by
  exactly 1,950 (2024) or 2,000 + 6,000 (2025), both figures from the record's
  deduction table and Schedule 1-A lines. Tax is 0 either way.
- `tenforty-b72.74`: the graph has no ND-1 line 6 exclusion
  (40% of net long-term capital gain, 4,000 here). Taxable income is high by
  exactly 4,000; tax is high by 1.95% of 4,000 (78.00) less the table
  half-row offset, bound 77.5125 +/- 0.505.
- Exact matches elsewhere: every zero-tax return (Single 40,000 and 60,000, MFJ
  with and without dependents, HoH) matches on both quantities. Dependents do
  not change line 20 (record scenarios 6 and 7).

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from each
defect's mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the observed
residual.
