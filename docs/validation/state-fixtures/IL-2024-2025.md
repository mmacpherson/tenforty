## Exemption repair (2026-11-04, b72.50)

The original blind record and its 28 return outcomes are unchanged. The graph
now computes the filer/spouse base plus all federally claimed dependents at
$2,775/$2,850 per person, with the strict federal-AGI exceptions from p.8. Exactly
$250,000 (non-MFJ) or $500,000 (MFJ) retains the allowance; the next whole dollar
removes all of line 10. State additions/subtractions do not change that federal
AGI test. Required entries and tax are rounded to whole dollars. The original
22 IL graph signatures reject exact matches when re-applied from the base
registry and are retired; NC/other-state signatures and all bounds are unchanged.
IL 2024/2025 enter graph scope using this official evidence.

`num_dependents` is the TOTAL count, not the CTC subset. `dependent_exemptions`
remains unmapped for IL. Existing `filer_65_or_older` and
`spouse_65_or_older` facts also reach the published $1,000-per-box age allowance;
the IL spouse box applies only to MFJ. The federal-AGI income exception removes
age and dependent allowances too. No new natural input was added. Claimable
filers/spouses and blind variants remain outside this ordinary-resident surface;
this change adds neither claimability nor blindness facts.
