### Permitted alternatives

None. Correction to the record (review, 2026-11-04): the record's ambiguity 1 mentions truncation, but the IL-1040 instructions (p.4) require cents of 50 to 99 to round up, so the record's half-up value is the only permitted value. The record itself is unchanged.

### Not pinned

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-03, after the record was committed)

- `tenforty-b72.50` (new): the graph applies no line 10 exemption allowance
  below the AGI limit, so taxable income equals base income; signature exact
  on taxable income ($2,775/$2,850 per person), tax within that × 4.95% ± $1.
- Above the limit the graph matches taxable income exactly; tax keeps cents
  (`tenforty-b72.42`, ± $0.50).

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `GRAPH_DEFECTS`: the test
fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds
come from each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the
observed residual. IL stays out of graph scope (`tenforty.graph_scope`) while
any of these beads is open.
