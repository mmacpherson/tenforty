### Permitted alternatives

None; every return has one outcome (revised 2026-11-25 after review).

- 2025 scenarios 5 and 6 (MFJ $100,000): Form 2 line 12 directs filers to the
  ordinary income tax table, whose joint row gives 68,500 × .059 − 506 =
  3,535.50, entered as 3,536 under half-up or half-even. The DOR web page's
  marginal rates give 3,535 (record uncertainty 2); that is a cross-check, not
  a permitted reading, since it uses an unprinted 506.40 subtraction.
- 2025 scenario 11: each worksheet line (8, 10 and 12 included) is a
  whole-dollar entry, so the tax is 2,475. Rounding only the total (2,474,
  record uncertainty 1) carries cents across form lines no source permits; it
  is a cross-check only.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents where each worksheet line is a
  whole-dollar entry, and prices the marginal 4.7%/5.9% rates where line 12
  uses the table's rounded subtraction constants. Every wage-only scenario (1
  to 7, both years) is signed at its exact centre, the marginal figure from the
  record's rates and thresholds less the record's tax, ± $0.01. For 2025
  scenarios 5 and 6 the table gives 3,535.50 (entered 3,536) and the marginal
  computation 3,535.10, so the centre is −$0.90: 40 cents from the constants,
  50 cents from the whole-dollar entry.
- `tenforty-b72.7` (existing): the graph has no net long-term gain input on
  worksheet line 2 (`mappings.py`, MT `natural_to_node` is empty;
  `MTForm2_2025.hs` L2 is a bare input), so `long_term_capital_gains` stays in
  ordinary income. Taxable income (line 4) is high by exactly the record's
  worksheet line 3 gain taxed (10,000 / 9,250 in 2025 scenario 9, capped at
  taxable income / 60,000), and tax is the 4.7%/5.9% schedule on the whole
  income instead of the 3%/4.1% stack on the gain plus the schedule on the
  rest. The bound on tax is the marginal schedule at the record's page 1
  line 7 (record ordinary income plus the gain taxed) less the record tax,

  ± $1.90 (three rounded lines at $0.50 plus up to $0.40 of 2025 booklet
  constant). Cases: scenarios 9 to 11, both years. Sizes: 2024 scenario 10
  about +$180, 2024 scenario 11 about +$1,065.
- `tenforty-b72.72` (new): the graph omits page 1
  line 6, the $5,500 (2024) / $5,660 (2025) subtraction for a filer 65 or
  older; MT has no mapping and `mt_adjustments` is the only route. The federal
  age addition and the 2025 Schedule 1-A senior deduction do flow through
  (graph taxable income equals record line 3). Taxable income is high by
  exactly the subtraction; tax is the marginal schedule at record ordinary
  income plus the subtraction, less the record tax, ± $0.90 (half a dollar of
  whole-dollar rounding plus the booklet constant). Cases: scenario 8, both
  years. Size about +$325 in each year.
- Exact matches elsewhere: taxable income in every wage-only
  scenario.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
each defect's mechanism and the record's own rates, thresholds and amounts,
never from the observed residual.
