## Supported domain and input meanings

Full-year resident, ordinary tax method, base standard deduction; under65, not blind and not claimable by another taxpayer. No additions or other subtractions, no extra exemptions or other credits/taxes in the worked cases. MFS spouse takes the standard deduction. HOH/QSS filing eligibility is stipulated externally; the zero-dependent examples isolate status arithmetic and do not establish eligibility to elect those statuses.

- The federal subtraction is the lesser of **eligible federal income-tax liability** and the stepped Table4 cap. The stipulated liability is already after the worksheet’s prescribed adjustments; it excludes self-employment tax. Raw graphs also expose worksheet adjustments tested separately.
- Natural Python scalar and batch graphs map `num_dependents` directly as **total regular dependents**, with a taxpayer/spouse base derived from filing status. MFJ has base2; Single/MFS/HOH/QSS have base1. `dependent_exemptions` is unmapped for OR. Federal child-credit eligibility is separate from this Oregon count.
- Regular credit is $249 per exemption in2024 and $256 in2025, allowed through AGI $100,000 inclusive for Single/MFS and $200,000 inclusive for MFJ/HOH/QSS. It cannot create negative net tax.
- Additional severe-disability/qualifying-disabled-child exemptions, claimable-filer variants, the special MFS no-income-spouse exemption, age/blind/dependent standard-deduction variants, RDP/as-if and mixed-residency returns, amended-return worksheet exceptions and special tax methods are unsupported by this evidence/domain. No new natural fields or extra exemption inputs were added.
- These are state calculations with stipulated eligible federal liabilities. They do not establish federal correctness, dependent eligibility, or a refund including the2025 kicker or other refundable credits/payments.
