### Tax table cutoff and rounding (I24/I25 p. 38 and p. 13)
- "Use this Tax Table if your taxable income is less than $100,000. If $100,000 or more, use the Tax Computation Worksheet on page 44." (p. 38; line 12 instruction on p. 16 [2025])
- Rows are in $100 bands, [at least, but less than). The bottom rows are 0–20 → 0, 20–40 → 1 and 40–100 → 2.
- The official rows are consistent with tax = schedule(band midpoint), rounded to the nearest dollar. The booklet does not print this rule; I checked it against every row used below.
- Round to whole dollars: "Drop amounts under 50¢ and increase amounts from 50¢ to 99¢ to the next dollar" (I25 p. 13, "Rounding Off to Whole Dollars"). The worksheet result on line 12 is therefore rounded half-up.

### Formation of WI income (I25 pp. 13–15; I24 same)
Line 1 is federal AGI (1040 line 11). Line 2 is the Schedule I adjustment. Line 4 is additions (Schedule AD) and line 6 is subtractions (Schedule SB).
Line 7 is Wisconsin income, which drives the standard deduction table. Line 8 is the standard deduction and line 10 the exemptions. Line 11 is taxable income and line 12 is tax.
In these scenarios there are no adjustments, additions or subtractions, so line 7 = wages.
I did not fetch the Form 1 face itself (the 2025 fillable-form URL returned 404). The line numbering above comes from the line instructions.

### Exemptions (I25 p. 15–16 "Line 10"; STAT 71.05(23))
- Line 10a: $700 × (1 for single or HoH not claimable as a dependent, 2 for MFJ) plus $700 per dependent.
- Line 10b: $250 for each of you or your spouse who is 65 or older, but only if that person gets the $700 exemption on 10a.
- The $700 personal exemption is confirmed. The age-65+ amount is an additional $250 per person, not $700.
- Scenarios: Single $700, HoH $700 (no dependents), MFJ $1,400.

### Sliding-scale standard deduction
**How the booklet presents it.** The Form 1 instructions print no formula or worksheet. Line 8 says:
"Most people can find their standard deduction by using the Standard Deduction Table on page 35. Use the
amount on line 7 to find the standard deduction for your filing status" (I25 p. 15). The only worksheet printed
is the dependents' worksheet ($1,350 floor / +$450), which does not apply here. The table is on pp. 35–37, in $500 bands of line 7 income, [at least, but less than).

**Statutory formula (STAT 71.05(22)(dp)/(dt)).** The deduction is the indexed maximum minus a percentage of the
Wisconsin income that exceeds an indexed threshold, with a floor of zero:
- Single: 12%
- Head of household: 22.515%, but never below the Single amount. The table shows this floor: HoH equals Single from the 57,000 row on (2025) and from 56,000 (2024).
- MFJ and MFS: 19.778%

Base-year amounts (pre-indexing): Single 7,200 over 10,380; HoH 9,300 over 10,380; MFJ 19,010 over 21,360 and MFS 9,030 over 10,140 (post-2015 re-basing).
Indexing follows August CPI.

## Rounding notes, ambiguity and uncertainty
1. The SD table uses $500 bands of line 7 income, keyed on "at least / but less than". A wage of exactly 20,000, 60,000 or 150,000 falls in the band that *starts* at that value. A fixture engine using the exact formula instead of the table can differ by a few dollars. For example, the 2025 Single exact formula at 20,000 gives 13,560 − .12×450 = 13,506, but the table gives 13,476 at the midpoint 20,250. **The official form value is the table value.** Engines that apply the formula exactly will not match these SD fixtures.
2. The tax table computes at the $100 band midpoint, so an exact-schedule engine differs by up to ±$2–3 below $100k of TI. For example, 2024 S TI 6,212 gives 217.42 exact against 219 in the table. The fixtures use the table, which is what Form 1 requires.
3. At 400k Single, the p. 44 worksheet and the exact bracket sum differ by half a cent (22,750.02 vs 22,750.025, and 22,362.69 vs 22,362.695). Both round to the same dollar.
4. HoH with no dependents is not a realistic filing status under federal rules. The scenario was requested, and the numbers follow the HoH column with one $700 exemption.
5. The phase-out start thresholds in the parameter table are inferred, not published in the booklet. The fixtures do not depend on them.
6. I did not fetch the statute's HoH "not less than the single amount" clause verbatim; the fetch returned only a summary. The table itself shows the floor (the HoH and Single columns coincide from 57,000 in 2025 and 56,000 in 2024). This does not affect any fixture.
7. The 2024 rate schedule comes from a Wayback snapshot of the DOR FAQ, because the live page now shows 2025. It reconciles exactly with the official I24 p. 44 worksheet subtraction amounts and with the I24 tax-table rows.
8. Credits are excluded, as instructed: the itemized deduction credit, school property tax credit and the rest. Line 12 is gross tax only.
