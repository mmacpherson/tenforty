### Quantities

The graph reports RI-1040 **line 7** as `state_taxable_income` and **line 13a**
(line 10a plus lines 10b to 12b) as `state_total_tax` (`src/tenforty/mappings.py`,
`OTSState.RI`; `tenforty-spec/forms/RIForm1040_2024.hs` and `_2025.hs`, outputs
`L7` and `L13a`). The record's summary column is line 8. With no credits,
recapture, checkoff, use tax or mandate penalty (all zero in every scenario)
lines 8, 10a and 13a are equal, so the pinned tax is the record's line 8.

### Permitted alternatives

Four returns (2024 and 2025 scenarios 4 and 10, the Computation Worksheet
ones). The worksheets carry cents and no RI publication states a whole-dollar
rule for line 8 (record, Uncertainties 1), so each is listed as two whole
outcomes with the same line 7: the cent figure (primary: 5,614.25; 12,724.90;
5,566.00; 12,110.43) and the whole-dollar figure (5,614; 12,725; 5,566;
12,110). Every other return fixes its rule: the Tax Table is mandatory below
$100,000 and its rows are printed in whole dollars.

### Not pinned

None. Dependents are expressed with `num_dependents` and age with
`filer_65_or_older`; the graph ignores both for Rhode Island (and Schedule E
has no age box), which is part of the signed defect below, not a missing input.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

No case matches outright. The graph's line 7 is the Federal AGI less the
standard deduction by filing status, with nothing subtracted for exemptions,
so every return departs.

- `tenforty-b72.85` (new): line 6 is a raw `keyInput`
  (`RIForm1040_2024.hs` and `_2025.hs`, "Total exemption amount") with no route
  in `mappings.py` (`OTSState.RI` has an empty `natural_to_node`);
  `dependent_exemptions` is refused for RI and `num_dependents` is dropped
  silently. So the graph subtracts no personal, spouse or dependent exemption,
  even for a one-person return. Taxable income is high by exactly the
  Schedule E line 5 count times the exemption amount, from the record:
  1 x 4,950 (2025: 5,100) for scenarios 1 to 4, 8 and 9, 2 x for scenarios 5
  and 7, 4 x for scenario 6. Signature exact on taxable income. Tax is high by
  the rate schedule at the graph's line 7 less the schedule at the record's
  (the record's rate schedule, so the 4.75% bracket is used where the extra
  income crosses $77,450 / $79,900, as in scenarios 5 and 6), with the slack of
  the line 8 mechanism below added on each side. Typical size: $184 of tax on
  Single $40,000 in 2024 (1,104.38 against 920); $384 on MFJ $100,000, no
  dependents, in 2024.
- `tenforty-b72.86` (new, folded into the exemptions signature on
  scenario 10): above Modified Federal AGI of $246,450 (2024) / $254,250 (2025)
  the Standard Deduction and Exemption Worksheets reduce both amounts (record,
  scenario 10: 20% of each remains in 2024, 40% in 2025). The graph has the
  full standard deduction (`byStatusE` on filing status, no worksheet) and no
  exemption. Taxable income is therefore low, net, by
  4,230 + 3,960 - 21,150 = -12,960 (2024) and 8,720 + 8,160 - 21,800 = -4,920
  (2025), exact. The bead names one mechanism per signature, so scenario 10 is
  signed under the exemptions bead with the phase-out in the reason; fixing
  either alone moves the departure out of its bound.
- `tenforty-tj2.20`: below $100,000 line 8 must come from the Tax Table row;
  the graph prices the rate schedule at line 7 itself (`bracketTax` on `L7`).
  A row is the 3.75% schedule at the $50 row midpoint to the nearest dollar,
  so the slack is rate x half a row plus the half dollar: 0.0375 x 25 + 0.50 =
  1.4375. Scenarios 1 to 3 and 5 to 9, both years.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: at or above $100,000 line 8 is the worksheet result, and
  the whole-dollar reading is permitted; the graph keeps the cents. Slack
  $0.50. Scenarios 4 and 10, both years.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in the module's `DEFECTS`: the
test fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.
Bounds come from each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures (the
omitted exemption count x amount, the phase-out shares, the schedule rates and
limits, the $50 row, the whole-dollar rule), never from the observed residual.
RI stays out of graph scope (`tenforty.graph_scope`) while any of these beads
is open.

### Not covered by the record

No married filing separately or qualifying surviving spouse return (the graph's
MFS standard deduction is 10,550 / 10,900 where the record gives 10,575 /
10,900 for 2024 / 2025, so 2024 MFS would differ by $25), no Schedule M
modification, no credit, no dependent-of-another filer (exemption zero), no
return at or near $100,000 of taxable income, where the record's source wording
is inconsistent (Uncertainties 4).
