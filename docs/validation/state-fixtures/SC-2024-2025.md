### Quantities pinned

`state_taxable_income` is SC1040 line 5 (`L5_sc_taxable_income`) and
`state_total_tax` is line 6 (`L6_sc_tax`, `mappings.py` `state_output_lines`).
These are the record's "SC taxable income" and "SC tax" columns, so every pin
is a direct transcription.

### Permitted alternatives

None. SC rounds to whole dollars, requires the table below $100,000 and the
schedule from $100,000, so each case has one outcome.

### Not pinned

- `SC-2024-s6` and `SC-2025-s6` (MFJ, children aged 2 and 4): the line t
  deduction for dependents under 6 needs a dependent's age, which no public
  input carries, and the inputs equal scenario 5's.
- Scenarios 5, 7 and 10 are pinned on the record's assumption that every
  dependent is 6 or older.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

The graph imports federal taxable income as line 1, accepts line 2 and line 4
only as dollar inputs no public field reaches, and prices `bracketTax` on the
spec brackets (2024: 0% to $3,460, 3% to $17,330, top rate above; 2025: 0% to
$3,560, 3% to $17,830). All 18 record cases and the two schedule cases depart, and each is a strict signature in
`DEFECTS`.

- `tenforty-s48h.66` (existing): num_dependents is dropped silently, so line w
  ($4,790 per dependent for 2024, $4,930 for 2025) is not subtracted; the 44%
  net capital gain deduction (line i, $4,400 on $10,000) is not either. Taxable
  income is high by the omitted amount, exactly.
- `tenforty-b72.82`: the $15,000 age-65 deduction (line q)
  is not applied for `filer_65_or_older`. Taxable income is high by $15,000
  (2024) or, in 2025, by $15,000 less the $6,750 add-back the graph also omits.
- `tenforty-b72.81`: the 2025 graph makes no
  Information Letter 26-4 add-back, so taxable income is low by the add-back
  ($750 Single, $1,500 MFJ).
- `tenforty-b72.80` (open, thresholds corrected): the 2024 spec brackets used
  3,560 and 17,830, which gave a schedule constant of $677.36 against
  SC1040TT's printed $659. The 2024 SC1040TT prices the first non-zero row at
  3% above $3,460, and the brackets now use $3,460 and $17,330. They imply a
  constant of $658.36, not the printed $659, so at and above $100,000 (where
  the form's rule is 6.2% x line 5 less 659) the graph is $0.64 high before
  rounding: the worked example (line 5 $101,000, tax $5,603; SC1040TT 2024
  p.4) comes out $5,603.64. Every 2024 tax signature carries this $0.64 in its
  centre under `tenforty-b72.80`; table-row slack applies only below $100,000.
  The printed-constant fix itself is left for a later change.
- Two schedule cases are pinned for 2024, Single, signed under b72.80:
  `SC-2024-pub1` (line 5 $101,000, tax $5,603; evidence kind published worked
  example, SC1040TT 2024 p.4) and `SC-2024-cut` (line 5 $100,000, tax $5,541;
  a source-verified application of the published p.4 schedule, which
  explicitly includes $100,000: 6,200 - 659 = 5,541, verified by Codex,
  2026-11-25; recorded under the `unverified-legacy` evidence kind because no
  existing kind says that, so it is not claimed as an independent derivation).
  Both are signed at exactly +$0.64 (within a cent) under b72.80, with no
  whole-dollar or row slack, since the official results are whole dollars and
  the only signed mechanism is the 659 - 658.36 constant. Both use public
  inputs: SC line 5 is federal taxable income, so wages are line 5 plus the
  $14,600 federal standard deduction.
- `tenforty-tj2.20`, `tenforty-b72.42`: below $100,000 the form requires the
  $100 table row, which prices the row midpoint, and the graph prices the exact
  income; line 6 is also a whole-dollar entry. These widen each tax bound.

Each tax bound is: top rate times the omitted line 5 amount, plus the published
schedule constant ($659 or $642) less the spec's ($658.36 or $642.00, from the
spec brackets: top rate times the upper threshold less 3% of the 3% band's
width), with slack of $0.50
for the whole-dollar rule and, for table rows, top rate times $50 for the row
midpoint. Taxable income bounds are the record's line 1 less its line 5. None
is taken from a residual.

Open point for adjudication: the 2025 add-back rests on Information Letter 26-4,
which the record says carries no precedential value and whose force depends on
the legislature not having conformed (record Uncertainty 1). If SC conformed
for 2025, variant B is right, the add-back signature is wrong, and the 2025
cases need re-pinning.
