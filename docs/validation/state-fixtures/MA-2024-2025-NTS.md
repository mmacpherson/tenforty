## Legal worksheet and restricted implementation

The legal AGI worksheet floors Form 1 line 10 plus the abandoned-building renovation addback, subtracts only Schedule Y lines 2–9b and 10 (again floored), then adds Schedule B35 (or Form 1 L20 when the instructions require that fallback) and nonnegative Schedule D19. The graph implements only the zero-investment/capital, supported ordinary-income domain: it uses the first component and does not compute general B35/D19. The Single threshold is $8,000; MFJ is $16,400 plus $1,000 per dependent; HoH is $14,400 plus $1,000 per dependent. Equality qualifies. MFS is ineligible. Massachusetts has no QW form status; its federal-to-state equivalence is unresolved under r91.9, so this change grants no new QW qualification.

Line 27 ordinarily zeros tax and skips lines 29–31. Recapture/installment taxes on lines 25/26 survive, and the instructions then allow completion of line 31, not lines 29/30.

## Restricted activation, not a tax-law exclusion

The graph does not compute Massachusetts Schedule B or D. Existing MA raw L20/L23a/L23b/L24 are taxable buckets, not established pre-exemption B35/D19 facts. Schedule B35 precedes the B36 excess exemption; B38/B39 subsequently feed Form 1 L20/L23. Schedule D19 precedes D20; D21 is taxable income and D22 is the tax transferred to official Form 1 L24. The graph's simplified L24 is itself multiplied by a rate. It must not be relabelled D19 merely to enable No Tax Status.

Consequently NTS activates only when all represented raw MA and federal investment/capital facts are zero. Signed facts are checked individually by their absolute values: short-/long-term cancellation and gains/carryover cancellation do not establish absence of capital activity. Federal facts need checking because the public graph lowering currently places them in federal income without populating the MA capital buckets. Derived federal capability outputs sum individual magnitudes: Schedule D CapitalFactsMagnitude, Schedule 1 IncomeAdjustmentMagnitude, and Form 1040 NonWageIncomeMagnitude. MA imports the latter rather than exporting all individual federal raw facts. Existing federal tax lines, inputs and public meanings are unchanged; only derived capability computations/outputs are added. There are no new B35/D19 inputs, presence bits, or override/fallback facts.

W-2-only threshold fixtures establish the resolved case. Eligible Schedule Y and abandoned-building addback facts remain raw-only; ordinary Form 1 aggregate deductions are not substituted for that subset. Existing dependent-exemption dollars carry the $1,000-per-dependent threshold increment. The inherited MA line-10 federal-AGI approximation remains tenforty-b72.39. NTS activation additionally requires every represented non-W-2 federal income and Schedule 1 adjustment fact to be zero, including the computed self-employment-tax deduction. Individual absolute amounts prevent cancellation from hiding unsupported facts. This deliberately conservative domain does not assert that those income types are legally ineligible for NTS.

For example, the 2024 Form 1 instructions p.10 and 2025 instructions p.12 disallow IRA/Keogh/SEP/SIMPLE contribution deductions for MA. Wages $8,100 with a federal IRA deduction $200 produce federal AGI $7,900, but cannot establish MA NTS. The guard retains the previous non-NTS computation ($175 under the inherited proxy); that preservation assertion is not a claim that $175 is the fully reconciled legal MA tax. Repairing the income proxy remains b72.39. Outside this supported ordinary-income domain the existing computation is unchanged, as it is outside the capital-free domain.

`NTS_ScheduleY` supplies MA-only eligible Schedule Y deductions not already
subtracted federally. It must not duplicate a federal adjustment; the guard
excludes any nonzero federal adjustment even if its net sum is zero. The
existing aggregate ordinary MA-deductions input is not this worksheet fact.

### Unrepresented state-wage limitation: b72.39 / b72.40

The supported fixtures assume federal W-2 box 1 equals Massachusetts wages.
The Form 1 line 3 instructions instead require MA wages (generally box 16)
for state/local employees with pension pickup contributions; those wages can
exceed the federal amount. The current graph has no separate state-wage fact,
so the W-2-only guard cannot detect §414(h) pickups or other unrepresented
federal wage exclusions that MA does not adopt. Thus it certifies the
represented regular-wage model, not every legally W-2-only return. A box-1 /
box-16 difference can still cause false NTS qualification. This inherited
input/proxy limitation is recorded on both b72.39 and b72.40; it is not claimed
resolved here. No blanket fail-closed guarantee applies to unrepresented
state-wage differences.

Outside the capital-free domain, the previous non-NTS calculation is retained. The structural regression with wages $7,500 and raw L24 gains $1,000 must retain $205 (5% × ($7,500 − $4,400) + 5% × $1,000), rather than silently becoming zero. This is preservation of the simplified graph calculation, not an independent full legal-value claim.

## Durable deferred witness: tenforty-b72.40

For Single in either year, wages $7,500 and ordinary dividends $100, no other income, losses, carryovers, or eligible Schedule Y deductions: worksheet wage component $7,500; B35 $100; D19 $0; total $7,600 ≤ $8,000. No excess personal exemptions are available against the investment amount because wages exceed the $4,400 personal exemption. The statutory NTS rule requires line 28 = $0. The narrowed graph deliberately retains non-NTS tax; a strict-xfail witnesses this limitation until b72.40 implements the general capital worksheet. This derivation is non-blind and is not labelled an independent whole-return fixture.
