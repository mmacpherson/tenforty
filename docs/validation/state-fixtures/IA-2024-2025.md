### Lines pinned (revised 2026-11-25, after review)

The graph reports IA 1040 **line 4** as `state_taxable_income` and **line 20**
as `state_total_tax` (`src/tenforty/mappings.py`, IA outputs
`L4_ia_taxable_income` and `L20_total_state_and_local_tax`). Line 20 is tax
after the Step 3 exemption credits, so the pinned tax is the record's
**"Line 12"** column (line 7 less the line 8 credits; lines 6, 9, 10 and the
surtax are zero in the record). The earlier pin at the record's "Line 5"
column (before credits) encoded the missing credit as correct behaviour and is
retired; the line 5 figures remain only in the record and in the arithmetic
below.

### Permitted alternatives

Iowa makes whole-dollar rounding optional (expanded instructions p.3, both
years): a filer may round every line half up, and the Department computes
adjustments to the cent. Both presentations are legal, so each return whose
line 12 differs between the two permits two outcomes, the record's cent-exact
line 12 first (the primary) and its whole-dollar line 12 second, with the same
line 4. Alternatives are accepted only as exact matches; the signed gap below
is measured from the primary.

### Not pinned

None. Every record scenario is expressible: `w2_income`,
`long_term_capital_gains`, `num_dependents` (s6, s7) and `filer_65_or_older`
(s8; for 2025 the graph's federal return already carries the $6,000 senior
deduction, giving the record's 26,250).

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed; revised after review)

- Line 4 matches in all 16 returns other than s1, for both years.
- `tenforty-b72.66` (exemption credits unfed): the graph returns line 5, so
  `state_total_tax` is above the record's line 12 by exactly the record's
  line 8 credit. Signed strictly on `state_total_tax` for every return except
  s1, measured from the primary (cent-exact line 12): graph minus primary equals
  the credit, with half a cent of slack for the record's cent rounding. Spec
  input: `L8 exemption_credit`, which no public input reaches.

  | Return (both years) | Line 8 credit | Record source row |
  | --- | ---: | --- |
  | s2, s3, s4, s9 (Single) | 40 | summary table, "Line 8 credits" |
  | s5 (MFJ) | 80 | same |
  | s6 (MFJ, 2 children) | 160 | same |
  | s7 (HoH, 1 child) | 120 | same |
  | s8 (Single, 65+) | 60 | same |

  s1 has no gap: line 5 is zero and line 12 is floored at zero, so the graph's
  0 equals the record's line 12 of 0; no tax signature is attached.
- `tenforty-b72.65` (new, for the coordinator to file): in
  s1 (Single, wages $12,000) federal taxable income is zero, so IA 1040 line 2
  is federal AGI less the standard deduction, which "can be negative"
  (expanded instructions p.9, both years): -2,600 in 2024 and -3,750 in 2025.
  The graph's line 2 imports federal line 15 (`IAIA1040_2024.hs` and
  `IAIA1040_2025.hs`, "Line 2"), already floored at zero, so line 4 is 0.
  Signed on `state_taxable_income` only; the tax is 0 either way. The bound is
  the unabsorbed deduction from the record's own arithmetic: 14,600 - 12,000 =
  2,600 (2024) and 15,750 - 12,000 = 3,750 (2025), exact, graph minus record.
  The record flags this as its uncertainty U1 but cites the instruction text;
  the signature is strict, so it fails if the graph moves.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in the module's `DEFECTS`: the
test fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.
