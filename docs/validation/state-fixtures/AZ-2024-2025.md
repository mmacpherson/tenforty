### Permitted alternatives

None. Form 140 instructions p. 4 fix the rounding ("If 50 cents or more, round
up to the next dollar"), line 46 is the 2.5% rate with no tax table for 2023
and later, and the record's two branched scenarios turn on a fact, not on a
reading of the sources (see Not pinned).

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- `tenforty-b72.61` (new): the graph omits the line 49
  Dependent Tax Credit ($100 per dependent under 17, $25 per dependent 17 or
  older). The spec's credits are raw dollar inputs
  (`tenforty-spec/forms/AZForm140_2024.hs` lines 97-98, and the 2025 module)
  and `num_dependents` is not mapped for AZ (`src/tenforty/mappings.py`,
  `OTSState.AZ`), so the count is silently ignored. Line 52 is high by the
  record's line 49 amount: $200 (s5), $100 (s6), $225 (s9). Bound: exactly the
  line 49 amount where 2.5% of line 45 is a whole dollar, and that amount
  ± $0.50 where it has cents (the b72.42 mechanism below, on the same line 46).
  Six cases. Taxable income matches.
- `tenforty-b72.60` (new): the graph omits the line 38
  exemption for age 65 or over ($2,100 per box 8). The spec takes line 38 as a
  raw dollar input (`AZForm140_2024.hs` line 65) and `filer_65_or_older` is not
  mapped for AZ. Taxable income is high by exactly $2,100; tax is high by
  $2,100 × 2.5% = $52.50, ± $0.50 for the line 46 rounding. One case
  (2024 s7).
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents on line 46 (`l45 .*. rate`, spec
  line 93) where the form rounds every line to the whole dollar (instructions
  p. 4). Bound ± $0.50, applied only where 2.5% of the record's line 45 has
  cents: 2025 s1, s2, s3 (each $0.25 high) and 2025 s4 ($0.50 low; the form
  rounds 1,712.50 up).
- Exact matches elsewhere: 2024 s1 to s4, and taxable income in every case
  except 2024 s7.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in the module's `DEFECTS`: the
test fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.
Bounds come from each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures (the
line 49 amount, the $2,100 exemption, the 2.5% rate, the whole-dollar rule),
never from the observed residual. AZ stays out of graph scope
(`tenforty.graph_scope`) while any of these is open.
