dependents) is a separate record with its own tests; this document covers the
2026-11-11 record only.

### Permitted alternatives

Scenario 10, both years: the line 62 worksheet says "whole dollars only" and
the record found no statement of how to round the 1% product (uncertainty 1).
Nearest dollar is the primary outcome (line 64 of 129,678 for 2025, 130,258
for 2024); truncation, $1 lower (129,677 and 130,257), is also accepted, because the
cited instructions leave the rounding convention unstated.
Each is a whole outcome with the same line 19.

Line 31 has one outcome everywhere: the booklet requires the Tax Table at or
below $100,000 and the Tax Rate Schedule above it, and the schedule's Step 4
fixes its rounding. No schedule result in the record falls on $0.50.

### Not pinned

None.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Line 19 matches exactly in all 21 cases. Line 64 departs in all 21, by four
mechanisms; the two credit omissions sit on top of a line 31 precision term.

- `tenforty-tj2.20` (scenarios 1, 2, 4, 8, and the precision term of 5, 6
  and 7): at or below $100,000 the graph prices line 31 with `bracketTax` on
  the exact line 19 (`CA540_2024.hs`/`CA540_2025.hs`, line 49) instead of the
  mandatory Tax Table row, which is the schedule at the midpoint of a $100
  row, rounded to the dollar. Bound ± (row rate × $50 + $0.50), with the row
  rate taken from the record's midpoint cross-check (2% to 9.3%, so ± $1.50
  to ± $5.15).
- `tenforty-b72.42` (scenarios 3 and 10, and the precision term of 9 and 9b):
  above $100,000 the graph keeps the schedule's cents where Step 4 rounds the
  product to cents and the sum to the dollar; bound ± ($0.50 + $0.005).
  Scenario 10 also keeps cents on line 62 (`L62`, lines 92-99), a second
  whole-dollar line: bound ± ($1.00 + $0.005).
- `tenforty-b72.54` (scenarios 5, 6, 9, 9b): line 32 is personal credits only
  (`L32`, lines 51-68); the line 10 dependent credit is omitted, and
  `num_dependents` reaches only FTB 3514. Line 64 is high by the dependent
  amount the record allows: 2 × $475 = $950 and 2 × $461 = $922 (scenario 5),
  $475 and $461 (scenario 6), $950 for 2025 scenario 9, and the AGI
  Limitation Worksheet line m where the phase-out applies ($862 for 2024
  scenario 9, $866 for 9b). Bound: that amount ± the case's line 31 precision
  term above. The graph's phase-out of the personal credits agrees with the
  record's worksheet line i.
- `tenforty-s48h.44` (scenario 7): `filer_65_or_older` does not reach Form
  540, so the line 9 senior credit is omitted. Line 64 is high by $153 (2025)
  and $149 (2024), ± the Tax Table term.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in the module's `DEFECTS`: the
test fails if the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.
Bounds come from each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures (the
omitted credit, the row rate and half-width, the whole-dollar rule), never
from the observed residual. CA is held out of graph scope
(`tenforty.graph_scope`) on `tenforty-b72.54`, `tenforty-tj2.20`,
`tenforty-yu51` and `tenforty-nuc6`; `tenforty-b72.42` and
`tenforty-s48h.44`, signed here, are not yet on that list.
