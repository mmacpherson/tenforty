### Quantities pinned

| Result field | Graph node | TC-40 line | Record column |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

The graph's line numbers after line 16 are its own, not the form's. Its "L20"
is line 10 less the 6% credit and less an "other nonrefundable credits" input
that is zero here, so on these wage-only returns it is the form's line 22.

### Inputs

Wages are `w2_income`. Dependents are `num_dependents`: TC-40 line 2 counts the
dependents claimed for a federal dependent credit, and the record takes every
scenario dependent as one. The age-67 filer is `filer_65_or_older=True`.

### Permitted alternatives

None. The instructions fix the rounding (p. 3: whole dollars, 50 cents and
above up), so each line has one value. The record's Uncertainty 1 (whether the
2025 Schedule 1-A senior deduction belongs in line 12) is not an open reading:
the 2025 instructions take line 12 from federal line 12e, and the senior
deduction reaches Form 1040 on line 13b. The record's $1,238 for scenario 8,
2025 is therefore not listed as an outcome.

### Not pinned

None.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Line 9 matches in all 18 cases. Line 22 matches in one (2025 Single $20,000,
where the credit exceeds the tax and both sides are $0) and departs in 17.

- `tenforty-b72.83` (new): the graph has no TC-40 lines
  17 to 19. The form reduces the line 16 initial credit by 1.3% of income above
  the base phase-out amount ($17,652 / $26,478 / $35,304 for 2024; $18,213 /
  $27,320 / $36,426 for 2025). `tenforty-spec/forms/UTTC40_2024.hs` and
  `UTTC40_2025.hs` subtract the whole 6% credit (spec line 16, lines 66 to 68)
  from line 10 (spec "L20", lines 83 to 85), so the graph's tax is low by the
  credit the phase-out removes: record line 16 less record line 20. Eleven
  cases carry this signature alone (every no-dependent case but 2025 Single
  $20,000), from $31 (2024 Single $20,000) to $945 (2025 Single $150,000).
- `tenforty-b72.84` (new): the graph's line 11 is a raw
  graph input that no public input reaches. `STATE_GRAPH_CONFIGS[UT]` in
  `src/tenforty/mappings.py` has an empty `natural_to_node`, so
  `num_dependents` is accepted and ignored, and `dependent_exemptions` is
  refused as unsupported. The graph's credit is therefore 6% of federal line 12
  alone, short by 6% of line 11 ($2,046 or $2,111 per dependent). The six
  dependent cases (scenarios 6, 7 and 9) carry both departures in one
  signature under this id: the missing exemption raises the tax by 6% of line
  11 and the missing phase-out lowers it by line 16 less line 20.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents on lines 10 and 16 where the form
  enters whole dollars. It is inside the bound below rather than a signature
  of its own, and will surface by itself once the two defects above are fixed
  (2024 Single $85,000: line 10 is $3,867.50 before rounding).

How each bound was derived. Without line 11 and lines 17 to 19, the graph's
tax is line 10 less (line 16 less 6% of line 11), floored at zero. The
departure is that figure less the record's line 22, computed from the record's
own lines 10, 11, 16 and 22. The bound is that departure ± $1: lines 10 and 16
are each entered half-up in whole dollars, so a figure that keeps their cents
is within $0.50 of each. Where the unfloored figure is below zero and line 22
is $0, both sides are zero and there is no signature. No bound uses the
observed residual.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Fixing either
defect moves the six dependent cases out of their bound, which is the prompt
to re-derive their signature for the defect that remains. UT stays out of
graph scope (`tenforty.graph_scope`) while either defect is open.
