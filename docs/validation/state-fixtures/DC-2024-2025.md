### Permitted alternatives

None. The booklet fixes line 20 as the table row at or below $100,000, so the
record's schedule cross-checks are not alternatives.

### Not pinned

None. Scenario 10 (itemizer) is pinned and signed: its inputs are expressible
(`state_and_local_taxes` 8,000, `federal_itemized` 12,000) and the graph ignores
them for DC.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Four cases (scenarios 3 and 9 in both years, Calculation I at whole-dollar
income) match exactly. The other 16 depart by one of three mechanisms:

- `tenforty-tj2.20`: the graph prices the Calculation I schedule at line 19
  where the booklet requires the $50 table row (s1, s2, s4, s5, s6, s8, both
  years).
  Bound: from -(rate x $50 + $0.50) to +$0.50. The record shows the printed
  rows are the schedule evaluated inside the $50-wide row (up to $60,000, the
  schedule at the row midpoint; above, about $1 under it), so the schedule at
  the row's lower edge cannot differ by more than the rate times the width. The
  same bound is carried by every other table case below.
- `tenforty-b72.63` (fixed): `TablesDC2025.hs` carried the federal OBBBA
  amounts $15,750 / $31,500 / $23,625. The 2025 D-40 booklet (p. 4, p. 13) and
  the CFO letter keep DC at $15,000 / $30,000 / $22,500 and state DC does not
  follow the OBBBA increase, so the record was right; the table now carries the
  DC amounts and the signature is retired.
- `tenforty-b72.64` (s7, both years): the graph
  has no additional standard deduction for age 65, so line 19 is high by
  exactly $1,950 (2024) / $2,000 (2025). Tax bound: 6% of the shift, with the table-row
  widening.
- `tenforty-s48h.73` (s10, both years): the graph takes the standard deduction
  and ignores itemizing, so line 19 is the record's Calculation F deduction
  (12,000) less the graph's standard deduction ($14,600 in 2024, $15,000 in
  2025): exactly -$2,600 and -$3,000. Tax bound: 6.5% of the shift with the
  table-row widening.

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
the mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the observed residual.
The s7 and s10 shifts come from the record's own deduction amounts.
