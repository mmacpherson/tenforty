over-$100,000 rule. The record's other reading (the bracket sheet formula for

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

All 24 cases depart; every departure is a signed strict signature in `DEFECTS`.
Bounds come from each mechanism and the record's own figures (rate times row
half-width, the whole-dollar rule, the minus adjustments the record prints, the
omitted exclusion), never from the observed residual. The tax bound is
`rate x excess taxable income + (record adjustment - graph adjustment)
+ personal credits`
plus or minus `rate x 50 + 0.50`; scenario 5 has no table row, so its slack is
$0.50.

- `tenforty-tj2.20` (reused): the Regular Income Tax Table is required, but the
  graph prices the rate schedule at the exact taxable income, not the row
  midpoint. Every return under $100,001 carries it: slack `rate x 50 + 0.50`
  (3.4% for scenario 1, 3.9% otherwise). Typical size 2024 scenario 2: about
  +$1.
- `tenforty-b72.57` (new): the indexed bracket
  sheets lower the minus adjustment by $10 per $100 of NTI over $92,301 (2024) /
  $94,701 (2025) until $95,501 / $97,801. The graph prices a flat 3.9% minus
  $409.86 and has no band. Center is record adjustment minus $409.86 (for example
  $127.40 - $409.86 for scenarios 6 and 7, 2024). Cases: 4b and 4c 2024, 4c
  2025, 6 and 7 both years. Typical size: about -$280 (2024 scenario 6).
- `tenforty-b72.58` (new): for NTI of $100,001 or more the
  table prints "$3,811 (2025: $3,809) plus 3.9% of the excess over $100,000"; the
  graph keeps the 3.9% schedule minus $409.86 (2025 graph: the same 2024
  figure). Center is `(3,900 - base) - 409.86`. Case: scenario 5 both years.
  Typical size: -$320.85 (2024).
- `tenforty-b72.56` (new): the 2025 graph repeats the
