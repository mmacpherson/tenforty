### Permitted alternatives

None. The table is mandatory below $100,000 (packet p. 27) and the worksheet
from $100,000 (p. 38), and the packet states its own rounding rule, so each case
has one outcome.

### Not pinned

- Scenario 9, Single wages 60,000 plus long-term gain 10,000, both years: the
  record's figure assumes the gain does not qualify for the Oklahoma capital
  gain deduction (Schedule 511-A line 12). Whether it qualifies depends on the
  property's location or the company's headquarters, the holding period and
  Form 561; no input carries it and the two readings differ materially.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

The graph matches no case. Beads `tenforty-b72.78` and `tenforty-b72.79`
(fixed) came from this comparison; `tenforty-tj2.20` and `tenforty-b72.42` are
reused.

- `tenforty-b72.78`: the Oklahoma spec (`tenforty-spec/forms/OK511_2024.hs`
  and the 2025 twin) has no line 11 exemption: line 13 is line 11 minus the
  deduction only. The form allows $1,000 per exemption (taxpayer, spouse,
  dependents; packet pp. 8-10). `num_dependents` is accepted and ignored for OK
  (`StateGraphConfig` for OK has no input mapping). Taxable income is high by
  exactly $1,000 per omitted exemption: $1,000 (Single, s1-s4, s8), $2,000
  (s5, s7), $4,000 (s6). Signature: exact on taxable income. Tax is high by
  that amount times the 4.75% marginal rate (every case is above the top
  bracket threshold), $47.50 per $1,000.
- `tenforty-b72.79` (fixed): the spec's MFJ and HoH brackets (from a
  third-party site) put the 3.75% bracket's top at $12,200 and gave $4,395 on
  $100,000, where the packet's p. 38 worksheet prints $4,373. The packet's
  Tax Table (pp. 27-38) reproduces exactly with 3.75% up to $14,400 for MFJ,
  HoH and QW (all 2,000 $50 rows below $100,000 in each of the 2024 and 2025 packets), so
  `TablesOK2024.hs` and `TablesOK2025.hs` now carry $14,400 and cite the
  packet. The signature is retired; Single was already right.
- `tenforty-tj2.20`: below $100,000 the form requires the table row (50 wide)
  and the graph prices the schedule at the actual income.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents where the form rounds to the dollar.

Tax bounds, all derived from mechanism and the record's own figures, not from
the observed residual: graph minus record equals
`0.0475 x omitted exemption dollars` with half-width
$1.00 for whole-dollar rounding of the table value and the $100,000 anchor, plus
`0.0475 x 50 = $2.375` for the row width on the seven table cases (the
worksheet case s4 has no row). Example, 2024 Single s1: omitted $1,000, centre
$47.50, bound [$44.125, $50.875].

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching.

### Doubts about the record

- The table rows were read from `pdftotext` output and were not compared
  visually with the PDF pages (record, Uncertainties 2). The anchor step-back
  check agrees with all eight rows.
- 2024 page numbers for the rows are inferred (Uncertainties 3).
- The record's 2025 packet came from a `/current/` URL (sha256 recorded).
