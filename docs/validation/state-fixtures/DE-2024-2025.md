### Which lines are pinned

The graph reports PIT-RES **Line 23** (spec `L21_de_taxable_income`) as
`state_taxable_income` and the balance after the personal credits, **Line 33**
(spec `L30_de_total_tax`), as `state_total_tax` (`src/tenforty/mappings.py`).
The pinned values are the record's "Line 23 taxable income" and "Line 33 DE
tax" columns. The spec's line numbers do not follow the 2024 or 2025 form.

### Permitted alternatives

None. The sources fix both open-looking points: the table is mandatory below
60,000 and the schedule at 60,000 or more (instructions p. 8), and amounts "from
50 to 99 cents are increased to the next dollar" (p. 4), so the .50 results in
scenarios 5, 6 and 10a (5,154.50 and 6,474.50) round up. Each case has one
outcome.

### Not pinned

- **Scenario 10b** (combined separate, filing status 4, 2024 and 2025,
  Line 33 total 5,379): two returns share one form with per-column credits, and
  no public input selects status 4; the runner pins one return's outcome.
  Scenario 10a (joint, 6,255) is pinned.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

Line 23 matches in 18 of 20 returns. Line 33 matches in none, because the
graph omits the personal credits. All 20 are signed.

- **Personal credits and the age deduction, `tenforty-s48h.52`.** The spec
  takes Lines 27a and 27b (`L27a`, `L27b`) and the additional standard
  deduction (`L20b`) as raw inputs that no public input reaches
  (`tenforty-spec/forms/DEFormPITRES_2024.hs` and `_2025.hs`;
  `mappings.py` maps only `itemized_deductions` for DE). So the graph's line 33
  is before every personal credit, and `filer_65_or_older` adds no deduction.
  - Tax bound: the record's credit for the scenario (Lines 27a plus 27b: 110
    for s1 to s4, s9; 220 for s5, s7, s8, s10a; 440 for s6). Graph minus
    record equals that amount, widened as below.
  - Taxable income (s8 only): the record's Line 21 of 2,500 is omitted, so graph
    minus record is exactly +2,500. Its tax effect is 2,500 x 5.55%, both
    incomes lying in the 25,000 to 60,000 band, added to the s8 tax bound.
- **Rounding, `tenforty-b72.42`.** Line 24 is a whole-dollar entry; the graph
  keeps cents (for example 5,154.50). Bound: half a dollar either way, added to
  every tax bound.
- **Table rows, `tenforty-tj2.20`.** The form requires a 50-wide table row below
  60,000 (s1, s2, s7, s8); the graph prices the exact income. Within one row the
  schedule moves by at most 50 times the marginal rate (4.8% for s1, 5.55% for
  the others), so each table case's tax bound is widened by that amount.

The single signature on `state_total_tax` combines these as centre (credit, plus
the s8 deduction effect) plus or minus (0.5 + row width x rate for table cases).
Bounds come from the record's figures and the mechanisms, not from the graph's
residuals. Every departure is strict: the test fails if the graph leaves the
bound, and also if it starts matching.

### Doubtful in the record

- Personal credit wording (record, Uncertainties 3): the instructions' Line 27a
  sentence counts only dependents, while the form and the instructions' example
  count each taxpayer and spouse too. The record follows the example. The
  scenarios' Line 23 and Line 24 values do not depend on it; Line 33 does.
- The table rows were read from extracted text, not from the page image
  (record, Uncertainties 2); a visual spot-check of the five rows used would
  close it.
