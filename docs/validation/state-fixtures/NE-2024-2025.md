### Permitted alternatives

Nebraska fixes the line 15 method by filing method, not income: e-filers must
use the Tax Calculation Schedule, paper filers may use the Tax Table (or the
over-table worksheet above $75,360 / $77,760). The schedule value is primary.
Where the table or worksheet gives a different whole dollar, its net (line 15
less the same line 18 credit) is listed as a second whole outcome with the same
taxable income, accepted only as an exact match: 2024 cases 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8,
9 and 10, and 2025 cases 2 and 7.

### Not pinned

None. Every case keeps both quantities pinned. The other Nebraska credits (child
care, earned income) are outside the record, which stops at line 17 less
line 18, and are outside the graph's mapped inputs; they are not unpinned cases.
The record assumes every dependent qualifies on line 4c (its uncertainty 3).
