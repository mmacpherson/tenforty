# Blind derivation: NJ and VA resident income tax, wage-only cases

Derived only from official NJ Division of Taxation and Virginia Department of Taxation
documents fetched from the web on **2026-10-02**. No repository code, tables, tests or tax
software were consulted. Local copies of every source PDF (with `pdftotext -layout`
extractions) are in `src/` next to this file; SHA-256 hashes are listed below so the exact
documents can be re-identified.

"PDF p" = physical page index in the PDF file (1-based). "printed p" = the page number
printed on the page. For the NJ booklets, printed p = PDF p - 2. For the VA 2024 booklet,
printed "Page N" = PDF p - 6.

## 2. New Jersey rules used (cited)

The same rules appear in the 2024 and 2025 booklets at the same page positions unless noted.

**N1. Filing status.** "In general, you must use the same filing status as you do for
federal purposes." "If you are married and file a joint federal return, you must also file a
joint New Jersey return." Head of household: "You can use this filing status if you meet the
requirements to file as head of household for federal purposes."
NJ24-I PDF p7 (printed p5), Lines 1-5; NJ25-I PDF p8 (printed p6), Lines 1-5.
Federal Single -> NJ status 1; federal MFJ -> NJ status 2; federal HOH -> NJ status 4.

**N2. Filing threshold.** Gross income from everywhere more than **$10,000** (Single,
MFS) or **$20,000** (MFJ, HOH, QW). NJ24-I PDF p5 (printed p3); NJ25-I PDF p5 (printed p3);
restated at Line 29, NJ24-I PDF p22 (printed p20), NJ25-I PDF p22.

**N3. Regular exemption (Line 6).** "$1,000 exemption for yourself and your spouse/CU
partner (if filing a joint return)". NJ24-I PDF p8 (printed p6); NJ25-I PDF p8 (printed p6).
Lines 7 (65+), 8 (blind/disabled), 9 (veteran), 10-12 (dependents) do not apply under the
case facts. **Line 13** = sum of lines 6-12 (NJ24-I PDF p9, printed p7).
**Line 30** = amount from line 13 (NJ24-I PDF p25; NJ25-I PDF p25).

**N4. Income.** Line 15 = State wages from W-2 box 16 (NJ24-I PDF p10, printed p8).
Line 27 total income = line 15 + other income lines (form NJ24-F/NJ25-F p2). Line 28a/28b
retirement exclusions: line 28a applies only to pension/annuity income (none here); line 28b
(Worksheet D, NJ24-I PDF p23) is barred when wages exceed $3,000 ("Is the amount on line 8
MORE than $3,000? Yes. You are not eligible..."), and requires age 62+. So line 28c = 0.
Line 29 NJ Gross Income = line 27 - line 28c.

**N5. Deductions.** Lines 31-37c (medical, alimony, conservation, HEZ, alternative business
calc, organ donor, NJBEST, NJCLASS, tuition) - none apply. **Line 38** = lines 30 through 37c
(NJ24-I PDF p27, printed p25). **Line 39** Taxable Income = line 29 - line 38 (same page).

**N6. Property tax.** No property taxes or rent, so line 40a and line 41 are blank.
**Line 42** NJ Taxable Income: "If you did not claim a Property Tax Deduction, enter the
amount from line 39." NJ24-I PDF p33 (printed p31); NJ25-I PDF p33.

**N7. Tax (Line 43).** "If the income on line 42 is less than $100,000, use the Tax Table on
page 54. Otherwise, calculate the tax by using the Tax Rate Schedules on page 63. Use the
correct column or schedule for your filing status." NJ24-I PDF p33 (printed p31); NJ25-I
PDF p33. The table header repeats: "Use this table if your New Jersey taxable income on line
42 is less than $100,000" (NJ24-I PDF p56 / NJ25-I PDF p56, printed p54). Table columns:
"1 or 3" (Single, MFS) and "2, 4, or 5" (MFJ, HOH, QW). Form line label: "43. Tax on amount
on line 42 (Tax Table page 54)" (NJ24-F p3, NJ25-F p3).

**N8. Rate schedules (cross-check only).** NJ24-I PDF p65 (printed p63) and NJ25-I PDF p65
(printed p63), identical rates for both years:
- Table A (Single/MFS): 0-20,000 x .014; 20,000-35,000 x .0175 - 70.00; 35,000-40,000
  x .035 - 682.50; 40,000-75,000 x .05525 - 1,492.50; 75,000-500,000 x .0637 - 2,126.25; ...
- Table B (MFJ/HOH/QW): 0-20,000 x .014; 20,000-50,000 x .0175 - 70.00; 50,000-70,000
  x .0245 - 420.00; 70,000-80,000 x .035 - 1,154.50; 80,000-150,000 x .05525 - 2,775.00; ...

**N9. Rounding.** Whole-dollar rounding is optional; 50 cents or more rounds up (NJ24-I /
NJ25-I PDF p6, printed p4). All inputs here are whole dollars; table values are whole dollars.

## 4. Virginia rules used (cited)

**V1. Filing status.** "In most cases, your filing status will be the same as the one you
selected on your federal return." 1 = Single, 2 = Joint, 3 = Married filing separately.
VA24-I PDF p14 (Page 8); VA25-I PDF p15 (Page 9). Federal Single -> 1; federal MFJ -> 2.

**V2. Filing threshold.** Must file (and tax is computed) if VAGI is at or above:
Single **$11,950**; MFJ combined **$23,900**. If Line 9 VAGI is below, tax is $0.
VA24-I PDF p9 (Page 3) and PDF p17 (Page 11); VA25-I PDF p9 (Page 3) and PDF p17 (Page 11).

**V3. Lines 1-9.** Line 1 = federal AGI; Line 2 additions, Lines 4-7 subtractions (age
deduction requires birth on/before Jan 1, 1960 for 2024 / Jan 1, 1961 for 2025 - not
applicable to under-65 taxpayers in these cases; no Social Security, refunds, or
Schedule ADJ items). Line 9 VAGI = Line 3 - Line 8. VA24-I PDF p15 (Page 9); VA25-I PDF p15.

**V4. Standard deduction (Line 11).** Must be claimed if federal standard deduction was claimed.
- 2024: Filing Status 1 **$8,500**; Filing Status 2 **$17,000**. VA24-I PDF p17 (Page 11);
  also "What's New" VA24-I PDF p7.
- 2025: Filing Status 1 **$8,750**; Filing Status 2 **$17,500** ("Legislation enacted during
  the 2025 General Assembly session increased the standard deduction for Taxable Years 2025
  and 2026 from $8,500 to $8,750 for single filers and from $17,000 to $17,500 for married
  filers filing jointly"). VA25-I PDF p17 (Page 11); "What's New" VA25-I PDF p7 (Page 1).

**V5. Exemptions (Line 12).** Section A: "Multiply the sum of exemptions claimed in the 'You,'
'Spouse' and 'Dependents' boxes by **$930**." Section B (65+/blind, $800 each) not applicable.
VA24-I PDF p15 (Page 9); VA25-I PDF p15 (Page 9).

**V6. Line 14** = Lines 10 + 11 + 12 + 13; **Line 15** Virginia Taxable Income = Line 9 -
Line 14. VA24-I PDF p17 (Page 11); VA25-I PDF p19 (Page 13).

**V7. Tax (Line 16).** "To compute your tax, you can use either the tax table or the tax rate
schedule on Page 35". VA24-I PDF p19 (Page 13); VA25-I PDF p19 (Page 13). Form label: "16.
Amount of Tax from Tax Table or Tax Rate Schedule (round to whole dollars)" (VA24-F p2,
VA25-F p2). Table: "The tax table can be used if your Virginia taxable income is listed in
the table. Otherwise, use the Tax Rate Schedule." The table ends at 98,356 in both years.

**V8. Tax Rate Schedule** (identical 2024 and 2025): not over $3,000: 2%; $3,000-$5,000:
$60 + 3% of excess over $3,000; $5,000-$17,000: $120 + 5% of excess over $5,000; over
$17,000: $720 + 5.75% of excess over $17,000. Worked example rounds $4,917.50 to $4,918.
VA24-I PDF p41 (Page 35); VA25-I PDF p41 (Page 35); VA25-T PDF p1.

**V9. Rounding.** "All amounts entered on your return must be rounded to the nearest dollar.
Amounts less than 50 cents should be rounded down while all amounts of 50 cents - 99 cents
should be rounded up." VA24-I PDF p15 (Page 9); VA25-I PDF p15 (Page 9).

**V10. Spouse Tax Adjustment (Line 17) and Net Amount of Tax (Line 18).** Filing Status 2
couples may reduce tax by up to $259 "if both have taxable income to report and their
combined taxable income on Line 15 is more than $3,000." Line 18 = Line 16 - Line 17.
STA Worksheet: per spouse, Line 3 = separate VAGI - ($930 + $800 x age/blind boxes); "If
either amount is 0 or less, stop here; you do not qualify"; Line 5 = smaller Line 3: "If this
amount is larger than $17,000 and Line 4 [joint taxable income] is larger than $34,000, skip
to Line 12 and enter $259". VA24-I PDF p18-19 (Pages 12-13); VA25-I PDF p18-19.

## 7. Ambiguities and how they were resolved

**A1. NJ wages vs federal wages.** NJ Line 15 uses W-2 box 16 State wages, which can
differ from federal wages (e.g., NJ includes non-401(k) retirement deferrals and some cafeteria
plan amounts; NJ24-I PDF p10). The case says the only income is W-2 wages and federal AGI
equals wages; I took NJ state wages = the stated wage amount.

**A2. NJ table vs schedule.** Below $100,000 the table is mandatory ("use the Tax Table";
"you must use the Tax Rate Schedules" only at $100,000 or more). The table is built at the
midpoint of each $50 row, so at a row's lower edge it can exceed the exact schedule figure by
about $1-2 (case 2: schedule 1,767 vs table 1,769; case 4: 2,695 vs 2,696; case 5: 1,215 vs
1,216). I used the table, as the instructions require.

**A3. NJ case 4 sits just under the table cutoff.** Taxable income 99,000 < 100,000, so the
table applies; a $1,000-higher taxable income would switch to the schedule.

**A4. VA table vs schedule.** VA expressly allows either ("you can use either the tax table
or the tax rate schedule"; form label "from Tax Table or Tax Rate Schedule"). The table is
optional and built at row midpoints, so it can differ by $1 (cases 7 and 8). I report the rate
schedule figure as primary because it is the exact computation that the table approximates,
it is the only method the 2025 booklet itself prints (VA25-I PDF p41 says "A detailed tax table
can be found at www.tax.virginia.gov"), and it is the only method available above $98,356 (case 9).
The table figure is given alongside so a consumer can choose. A test expecting the
schedule figure should allow $1 tolerance if it means to accept table-based returns too.

**A5. VA Spouse Tax Adjustment for MFJ.** Case facts do not say how the joint wages split
between spouses, and STA (Line 17) depends on that split (0 if one spouse has no income;
$259 for any split where the smaller spouse's VAGI minus $930 exceeds $17,000). STA is an
adjustment on the tax computation, not a payment or refundable credit, so it arguably falls
inside "liability before payments/refundable credits." I report Line 16 as the tax line
(determinate from the facts) and give Line 18 for both the single-earner case (= Line 16) and
an illustrative 50/50 split.

**A6. NJ HOH with no dependents.** Federal HOH normally needs a qualifying person; the case
says "no dependents." A qualifying person need not be a claimed dependent (e.g., a parent the
taxpayer supports may count; also some HOH qualifying children are not dependents). I took
the status as given and claimed no dependent exemptions (lines 10-12 = 0), per the case facts.

**A7. NJ age 62-64 retirement exclusion.** "Under 65" could include 62-64, which is the age
