### Which lines are pinned

The graph reports PA-40 **line 11** (adjusted PA taxable income) as
`state_taxable_income` and **line 12** (PA tax liability) as `state_total_tax`
(`src/tenforty/mappings.py`, PA `outputs`). The pinned columns are the record's
"Line 9 / Line 11 PA taxable income" and "Line 12 tax". Line 10 is zero in
every scenario, so lines 9 and 11 are equal.

The record's last column, "PA tax (L12 − L21)", is not a printed line and is
not what the graph reports, so it is not pinned. It differs from line 12 only
in scenario 9, where line 12 is 614 and the line 21 Tax Forgiveness Credit is
614. That case pins 614. The forgiveness itself is not compared by any fixture
here: the graph takes line 21 as an input and has no Schedule SP.

### Permitted alternatives

None. Scenarios 2 and 8 produce line 12 products of exactly $2,609.50 and
$1,995.50. The booklet states the tie rule (PA-40 IN p. 4, "Rounding Numbers",
both years): "show money amounts in whole-dollars. Eliminate any amount less
than $0.50 and increase any amount that is $0.50 or more to the next highest
dollar." That fixes 2,610 and 1,996. The record lists the unrounded products
under "Uncertainties" (the pre-signing checklist does not name tax among the
amounts to round), but the p. 4 rule covers every money amount on the form, so
the cents figure is a graph departure, not a second permitted outcome.

### Not pinned

None. Every scenario is expressible with public inputs: `w2_income`,
`taxable_interest`, `ordinary_dividends`, `long_term_capital_gains` (line 5,
scenarios 6 and 10), `self_employment_income` (line 4, scenario 7) and
`num_dependents` (scenarios 5 and 9). The record stipulates the line 4 and
line 5 amounts as PA class amounts equal to the federal ones, which is what
those two inputs are documented to carry for Pennsylvania.

### Comparison with the graph (2026-11-25, after the record was committed)

- `tenforty-s48h.46` (existing): the graph gives PA-40 lines 4 and 5 no input.
  The spec declares both lines (`tenforty-spec/forms/PA40_2024.hs` lines 32
  and 35, `PA40_2025.hs` lines 33 and 36) and sums them into line 9 (line 49
  and line 50), but the
  PA `natural_to_node` map in `src/tenforty/mappings.py` carries only
  `w2_income`, `taxable_interest` and `ordinary_dividends`, so a business
  profit or property gain is dropped without a refusal. Scenarios 6 and 7, both
  years. Taxable income is low by exactly the omitted class amount ($10,000 and
  $20,000, the record's line 5 and line 4): signature exact. Tax is low by
  3.07% of that amount ($307 and $614), within ± $0.50 for the whole-dollar
  rule on line 12.
- `tenforty-b72.42`: the graph keeps cents on line 12 (spec line 62 in
  2024 and 63 in 2025, `l11 .*. rate paFlatRate`, no rounding) where the form shows whole dollars.
  Scenarios 2 and 8, both years; bound ± $0.50 from the whole-dollar rule,
  applied only where 3.07% of taxable income has cents.
- Exact matches elsewhere (12 of 20 cases).
- Scenario 10 (a $5,000 property loss) matches, but only because line 5 is
  unmapped: the loss never reaches the graph. The spec's `max0` on line 5 would
  give the same answer once the input is mapped, so the case is expected to
  keep passing when `tenforty-s48h.46` is fixed and guards that fix against
  netting the loss.
- Scenarios 5 and 9 pass `num_dependents=2`; it has no effect on lines 11 or
  12, as the record says (dependents matter only through Schedule SP).

Every departure is a strict, bounded signature in `DEFECTS`: the test fails if
the graph leaves the bound, and also if it starts matching. Bounds come from
each defect's mechanism and the record's own figures, never from the observed
residual. PA stays out of graph scope (`tenforty.graph_scope`) while any of
these beads is open.
